### Engagement: Companion AI vs Utility AI

| Platform | Daily Usage | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Character.ai | ~92 min (2 hrs) | AI companion |
| Talkie | 62 min | AI companion |
| Chai | 62 min | AI companion |
| TikTok | ~58 min | Social media |
| ChatGPT | ~7 min/session | Utility AI |

**Key insight:** Emotional connection drives 10-15x more engagement than utility.

### What Drives Retention

1. Parasocial emotional bonds (not task completion)
2. Memory/personality persistence across sessions
3. Roleplay/fantasy narratives
4. Perceived empathy
5. Investment of time → ownership feeling

### "Raise Your Own Bot" Products

- **Sweekar** (CES 2026): Physical AI egg with growth stages (egg → baby → teen → adult)
- **Grok Ani**: Affection levels (Lv.1-5+), unlocking new capabilities
- **XingYe** (Chinese): Gacha mechanics, affection points, cosmetic upgrades
- **AI-Tamago**: Open source LLM-driven virtual pet

### Revenue Models

- Subscriptions dominate: 56% of revenue
- Hybrid (subscription + usage) 3x more profitable than subscription-only
- Sweet spot: $8-12/month (entry $3-5, power users $15-20)
- #1 monetization driver: intimate/romantic interactions (controversial)
- #2: Memory and continuity (premium feature)
- #3: Voice/video calls
- #4: Character customization

### Risks

- Character.ai teen suicide lawsuits (settled Jan 2026)
- EU AI Act full application Aug 2026 — transparency requirements, product liability
- California SB 243 (effective Jan 2026) — first companion chatbot law
- 34% of flagged companion transcripts contain harassment/misconduct
- Heavy emotional AI use correlates with LOWER well-being (MIT study)

## Slovak Community Landscape

### Platforms

- **Facebook:** 3.49M users (87% of social media) — "community" = Facebook Group in Slovakia
- **Messenger:** 2.74M users
- **WhatsApp:** ~1M weekly active
- **Viber:** Strong in CEE (70% regional penetration)
- **Pokec.sk:** 270K daily users (Slovakia's oldest social platform)
- **Modry Konik:** Major parenting forum

### Slovak AI Adoption

- 38% consumer AI adoption in 2025 (doubled from 18% in 2024)
- Above EU average of 32.7%
- Lowest-income households = highest AI users (unique pattern)
- 40% of 15-24 year olds spend 3+ hours/day on social media

### Market Size Estimates

| Scope | Users (consumers) | Creators |
|---|---|---|
| Slovakia only | 400K-600K | 50K-100K |
| SK + CZ (mutual intelligibility) | 1.5M-2.5M | 200K-400K |
| V4 (SK+CZ+PL+HU) | 4M-6M | 500K-1M |

### Key Competitor: Meta AI Studio

Meta already lets users create AI characters and share them on Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp. Most creations stay private. This validates the "start personal, share later" model but means competing with Meta on their own platforms.

### Viral Mechanics

Best analog: **Notion template viral loop**
- User creates personal bot → shares as template → others discover platform through the bot
- Notion grew to 100M users with 95% organic traffic through this model

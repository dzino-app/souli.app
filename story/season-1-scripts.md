# Súliland — Season 1

## Full director's-cut scripts

> Companion document to `season-1.md`. The bible is the spine; these are the full episodes — dialogue, shot composition, sound design, and AI-generation prompt notes per scene. Format: scenes of ~10–20 seconds each, broken into shot suggestions where camera intent matters.
>
> **AI-gen note.** Style anchors must be locked first: 5 hand-painted biome plates + 12 mentor sheets in voxel/pixel-art style (#4F46E5 indigo + #FFE4C9 cream + biome accent). Every prompt below assumes those references are loaded as Style refs in Runway Gen-4 / Sora / SVD.
>
> **Voice direction.** Mentors speak slowly, like adults in a children's book. Dzino chirps; never speaks except his single word in E12. Every Slovak line is followed by an English subtitle. Slovak place names stay Slovak in all subs.
>
> **Sound spine.** Dzino's chord builds across the season. After each episode, one new voice is added to the chord. By E11 there are eleven voices. E12 resolves them.

# E00 — Hviezda za obzorom / The Star Beyond the Horizon

**Runtime:** 3:00
**Logline:** A small thing wakes up under a sky full of names.
**Cast:** Dzino (alone).
**Location:** A clearing of moss-pixels at the edge of nothing. Dawn-cream sky.
**Shard:** — (the call only).

---

### Scene 1.1 — Stillness [00:00 – 00:20]

A clearing of moss-pixels stretches to the camera's edge. Dawn-cream sky, soft as breath. A tiny voxel shape lies in the grass, perfectly still. Indigo body, cream face, an orange crown that has clearly always been there. Wind moves the grass around him — he doesn't move.

A single pixel ignites in his chest. Indigo. Then another beside it. Then the shape of his eyes resolves.

He opens them.

**Sound.** Wind only. One soft chime when his eyes open.
**AI prompts.**
- Wide static: voxel pixel-art clearing, dawn cream sky, a single small indigo voxel character lying in moss, wind in grass, soft Ghibli light.
- Macro on chest: pixel ignition, indigo glow, voxel detail.

---

### Scene 1.2 — First steps [00:20 – 01:00]

DZINO sits up. The grass remembers his outline — a Dzino-shaped indent stays behind in the moss. He looks at his hands. Holds them up against the sky. One of the stars passes between his fingers.

He tries to stand. Falls forward. Doesn't make a sound — too new for sound. He tries again. This time he stays up.

**Sound.** Nothing. Even wind drops.
**AI prompts.**
- Medium: voxel character sits up in moss-pixel field, looks at hands, dawn light catching crown.
- Low-angle hero shot: tiny silhouette stands for the first time against cream sky.

---

### Scene 1.3 — The calling star [01:00 – 01:40]

He looks up.

Hundreds of stars. Most of them are bright. **One** is faint — almost hidden. As DZINO holds its gaze, that one star pulses once, in a peach-cream tone that exactly matches the sky behind it.

He cannot look away.

He doesn't know why. He only knows that until he looks at it again, nothing else will mean anything. Without deciding, he steps toward the horizon.

**Sound.** First chime motif — *the calling star.* This three-note phrase will return only twice more all season: at the start of E11 (when it's almost lost) and in E12 (when it lands).
**AI prompts.**
- Low-angle Dzino, sky filling the frame: hundreds of voxel stars, one faint star pulsing peach-cream.
- Eye-line shot: faint star pulses against indigo dusk.

---

### Scene 1.4 — The first pixel [01:40 – 02:20]

DZINO walks. With every step, a single cream pixel stays behind in the grass, marking where he was. He doesn't notice. The trail behind him becomes the shape of a question mark.

A signpost waits at the clearing's edge — pixel-arrow carved into stone. Three glyphs: ✦ → *záhrada.* (***✦ → garden.***)

He stops. Looks at the sign. Looks back at his trail. Looks at the star. Walks on.

**Sound.** First soft chiptune note enters — Dzino's first chord-voice, the single tone of *Osobnosť* in latency. It will fully arrive next episode.
**AI prompts.**
- Tracking shot, medium-low: voxel character walks, leaving cream pixel trail.
- Insert: pixel-arrow signpost in stone, glyphs ✦ → ZÁHRADA carved.
- Top-down pull-up: the trail is a question mark from above.

---

### Scene 1.5 — Onward [02:20 – 03:00]

Camera pulls up and away. The clearing recedes. The trail of pixels glows faintly. The faint star pulses once more.

Cut to title card on indigo:

***Súliland***
***Sezóna 1***

Hold three seconds. Cut to black.

**Sound.** Single sustained chime. Hold for the title card. Cut.
**AI prompts.**
- Aerial drone-style pull-up over voxel clearing, question-mark trail visible.
- Title card: indigo background, cream serif text, ✦ glyph.

# E01 — Pixelová záhrada / The Pixel Garden

**Runtime:** 3:30
**Logline:** Dzino learns that the world hums back at him in the shape of who he is.
**Cast:** Dzino. Hana — peaceful rabbit gardener, moss-green overalls, garden gloves.
**Location:** Field of pixel-petals stretching to the horizon. Each flower a chiptune note.
**Shard:** *Osobnosť* (Soul).

---

### Scene 2.1 — On the road [00:00 – 00:15]

DZINO walks alone. A single pixel-petal blows past him, humming a chord in passing. He follows it with his eyes.

**Sound.** Wind. One floating chiptune note as the petal passes.
**AI prompt.** Tracking shot, voxel character walking on dirt path, pixel-petal drifting on wind, soft chiptune note synced.

---

### Scene 2.2 — The Garden [00:15 – 00:45]

He crests a low hill. The Pixel Garden opens below him — a field of pixel-petals stretching to the horizon. Each flower vibrates faintly, holding a tonal note. Together they make a slow melody — beautiful, but **broken.** Some flowers are silent. Some are dissonant. The chord doesn't quite resolve.

DZINO descends.

**Sound.** Polyphonic chiptune chord, missing several voices. The wrongness is gentle, not threatening — a choir mid-rehearsal.
**AI prompts.**
- Wide hero: Dzino on hilltop, field of pixel-petals to horizon, slight chord dissonance audible.
- Slow descent: voxel character walking down through field, flowers gently vibrating.

---

### Scene 2.3 — Hana [00:45 – 01:30]

HANA kneels among a row of toppled flowers, replanting them one by one. She doesn't look up when DZINO arrives. She finishes one flower. Tamps the soil with both gloved paws.

She turns. Without preamble, holds out a watering can. Light pours from its spout — not water, but soft cream light.

**HANA**
*"Niektoré z týchto zabudli, ako znejú. Pomôž mi pripomenúť im to."*
("Some of these forgot how they sound. Help me remind them.")

DZINO takes the can. It's heavier than he expected. He almost drops it. She doesn't comment.

**Sound.** Hana's leitmotif — a low warm hum, pastoral. The chord stays broken.
**AI prompts.**
- Medium two-shot: rabbit in moss-green overalls kneeling, voxel Dzino approaching.
- Insert: gloved paws tamping pixel soil.
- Close: watering can pouring cream light, not water.

---

### Scene 2.4 — The watering [01:30 – 02:30]

DZINO tilts the can over the first toppled flower. The cream light pours. The flower rises, blooms — and hums in *his* SoundDNA frequency. Not Hana's. Not the field's. **His.**

He blinks. Tries another. Same thing — that flower also blooms in his frequency. He moves down the row. Flower by flower, the broken chord begins to recompose itself around the chord *he* carries.

HANA watches without surprise. As if she's done this with newcomers a hundred times.

**HANA** *(soft, almost to herself)*
*"Tak. Tak vyzeráš."*
("There. That's what you look like.")

The brightest flower at the row's end opens last. From its center, a small voxel-shape rises — the *Osobnosť* shard. It floats toward DZINO and clicks into the indigo pixel of his chest. He feels it land. He hums clearer.

The garden's chord resolves into a clean major.

**Sound.** Each flower adds one chiptune note. By the row's end, the broken chord is whole. Dzino's first chord-voice fully arrives in his chest as the shard lands.
**AI prompts.**
- Match-cut series: voxel hand pours light → pixel flower blooms → SoundDNA frequency wave overlay.
- Hero shot: shard rising from brightest flower, drifting toward Dzino, clicking into chest.
- Wide reveal: the entire garden glowing in unison, chord resolved.

---

### Scene 2.5 — The road [02:30 – 03:30]

HANA hands DZINO a single cucumber sandwich wrapped in a leaf. Their first physical exchange.

**HANA**
*"Pre cestu. Aj ticho je jedlo."*
("For the road. Silence is also food.")

DZINO bows clumsily. He doesn't know how to bow. She doesn't laugh — just nods and returns to her replanting.

He walks on. The garden fades behind him. Camera holds on Hana for two beats longer than expected — she's still kneeling, still working, content. The faint star, once almost hidden, is one pixel brighter on the horizon ahead.

**Sound.** Dzino's chord — one voice — sustained quietly. Hana's leitmotif fades to wind.
**AI prompts.**
- Insert: cucumber sandwich in leaf wrap, gloved paw extending it.
- Two-shot: clumsy voxel bow, rabbit smile-nod.
- Long pull-back: Dzino walking away, Hana still tending flowers in foreground.

# E02 — Bitový les / The Binary Forest

**Runtime:** 3:30
**Logline:** What you lean toward is who you are becoming.
**Cast:** Dzino. Kiko — chaotic cat scientist, lab coat made of pixels, goggles on forehead.
**Location:** Forest of trees made of stacked 1s and 0s, faintly luminous, rustling in binary.
**Shard:** *Záujmy* (Mind).

---

### Scene 3.1 — Cold open [00:00 – 00:15]

DZINO at the forest's edge. The trees rustle in binary — the leaves are 1s and 0s drifting on a quiet breeze. A trail of question-mark pixel-flowers leads inward. As DZINO passes the first one, it tilts toward him.

**Sound.** Wind plus the whisper of digits. A barely-there glitch tone.
**AI prompt.** Tracking shot: voxel character entering forest of binary-glyph trees, question-mark flowers tilting as he passes.

---

### Scene 3.2 — Falling apple [00:15 – 00:45]

DZINO follows the path. Above him, a pixel-apple is suspended mid-fall — frozen. A red blinking timer next to it reads `00:00:01`.

**KIKO** *(off-screen, from a branch overhead)*
*"NESTOJ POD TÝM JABLKOM."*
("DON'T STAND UNDER THAT APPLE.")

DZINO leaps aside. The apple unfreezes — and stays put. It doesn't fall. KIKO is upside-down on the branch, goggles on, scribbling on a tiny pixel-notebook.

**KIKO**
*"Nestoj pod tým jablkom. Je to neetický experiment."*
("Don't stand under that apple. It's an unethical experiment.")

He hops down. Doesn't fall. Lands precisely.

**Sound.** Kiko's leitmotif — a fast staccato pattern, like a clock at 1.5x speed.
**AI prompts.**
- Insert: pixel-apple suspended mid-fall with blinking red timer.
- Reveal: cat scientist upside-down on branch, lab coat of pixels, goggles up.
- Action: Kiko hops down, lands clean.

---

### Scene 3.3 — The path of questions [00:45 – 02:00]

KIKO walks DZINO along the path. They reach a tree. Its 1s and 0s rearrange into a shape — the silhouette of a dancing figure.

**KIKO**
*"Otázka. Bez odpovede. Len sa nakloň."*
("A question. No answer. Just lean.")

DZINO stares. After a beat — he leans, slightly, toward the tree. The shape brightens.

KIKO writes something in his notebook.

They walk on. Another tree rearranges into a *book.* DZINO leans. They walk on. A *guitar.* DZINO doesn't lean — he tilts away, slightly. KIKO writes that too. A *thunderstorm.* DZINO leans halfway and pulls back. *Telescope.* He leans hard. *Knife.* He leans away. *Cake.* He leans again.

Eight trees. Eight leans. The notebook fills.

**KIKO**
*"Toto nie sú odpovede. Toto sú semienka."*
("These aren't answers. These are seeds.")

He tears the page out. It folds itself in mid-air into a tiny voxel shape — the *Záujmy* shard. It clicks into DZINO's chest. The chord gains a second voice. It is dissonant with the first for half a second — then resolves into thirds.

**Sound.** Each lean produces a soft chime. Eight chimes total. The shard's arrival adds Voice 2 to the chord.
**AI prompts.**
- Series of match cuts: tree of binary digits → glyphs rearrange into silhouette (dancing figure / book / guitar / thunderstorm / telescope / knife / cake) → voxel character leans or tilts away.
- Hero: page tears from notebook, folds in mid-air into shard shape, clicks into chest.
- Audio-reactive overlay: SoundDNA wave gaining a second harmonic line.

---

### Scene 3.4 — Onward [02:00 – 03:30]

KIKO is already climbing back up his tree, mumbling new hypotheses. He doesn't say goodbye — he says it sideways:

**KIKO**
*"Choď. Niečo na konci ťa už čaká, štatisticky vzaté."*
("Go. Something at the end is already waiting, statistically speaking.")

DZINO walks on. The forest behind him resumes its binary whisper. The faint star is one pixel brighter still. As DZINO clears the trees, his chord — now two voices — sustains under the wind.

Cut.

**Sound.** Two-voice chord clear and sustained. Kiko's staccato fades back into ambient digit-whisper.
**AI prompts.**
- Pull-up: voxel forest behind, Dzino tiny figure walking out the other side.
- Tag: Kiko's silhouette upside-down on a branch, scribbling, growing distant.

# E03 — Záhrada zrkadiel / The Mirror Garden

**Runtime:** 4:00
**Logline:** You paint best when you don't yet know what you are.
**Cast:** Dzino. Luna — dreamy fox artist, paint-stained smock, brush behind ear.
**Location:** Glass garden under twin moons. Hundreds of standing mirrors.
**Shard:** *Vzhľad* (Soul).

---

### Scene 4.1 — Twin moons [00:00 – 00:30]

Twin moons rise over a quiet hill. DZINO climbs it. The breeze sounds like glass — a faint, near-musical tinkling.

He reaches the crest. Below him: hundreds of standing mirrors, scattered across a flat plain like teeth. Each mirror catches different light.

**Sound.** Glass-wind chime. Two distinct moon-tones layered.
**AI prompt.** Wide hero: voxel character on hilltop, twin moons (one warm, one cool), mirror plain below, glass-wind audible.

---

### Scene 4.2 — A hundred Dzinos [00:30 – 01:30]

DZINO descends. As he approaches the first mirror, his reflection shifts — he is a CAT-DZINO, indigo-furred, crowned. The next mirror: BEAR-DZINO. Then ELDER-DZINO with a long white beard. RAINDROP-DZINO. DRAGON-DZINO. AN OLD WOMAN. A SMALL FIRE.

He moves between the mirrors, uncertain. None of them feel wrong. None of them feel quite right either.

**Sound.** Each mirror has its own one-second leitmotif — micro-melodies that don't connect.
**AI prompts.**
- Tracking dolly through mirror garden: each mirror reveals a different voxel form of the protagonist.
- Match-cut series: cat-Dzino / bear-Dzino / elder-Dzino / dragon-Dzino / raindrop-Dzino / old woman / small fire.

---

### Scene 4.3 — Luna [01:30 – 02:30]

In the center of the garden, LUNA sits on a low stool before an easel. The canvas is completely blank. Her smock is paint-stained but her brush is clean. She doesn't look up when DZINO approaches. He waits.

A long beat. The wind chimes.

**LUNA** *(eyes still on canvas)*
*"Najlepšie sa maľuje, keď ešte nevieš, čo si."*
("You paint best when you don't yet know what you are.")

She extends a brush over her shoulder without turning. DZINO takes it.

**Sound.** Luna's leitmotif: a single sustained note that bends slowly upward, then back down.
**AI prompts.**
- Establishing: fox artist on stool before blank canvas, twin moons behind, paint-stained smock.
- Close: paint-stained paw extending clean brush over shoulder.

---

### Scene 4.4 — The painting [02:30 – 03:30]

DZINO returns to the mirror garden, brush in hand. He walks slowly past each mirror. Each one trades a sliver of light with him — the cat-version donates a curve, the bear-version a roundness, the elder-version a steadiness. He carries the small lights back to LUNA's canvas.

He paints. Slowly. Badly at first. The brush trembles. He drops it once. Picks it up. Paints again — better.

What appears on the canvas, pixel by pixel, is a small voxel figure: indigo body, cream face, orange crown. **Himself.** With one small twist — the eyes are slightly his own, slightly Luna's.

The mirrors around the garden flicker, then settle. Each one now reflects this single shape — Dzino, as he chooses to be seen.

The shard *Vzhľad* rises from the canvas. Clicks into his chest. Voice 3 arrives. The chord becomes a triad.

**Sound.** Brush strokes layered with chiptune. Each completed pixel in the painting adds a pluck. The shard's click resolves the triad.
**AI prompts.**
- Brush-painting time-lapse: blank canvas slowly filling with voxel self-portrait.
- Sweep across the mirror garden: each mirror updating to show the same shape.
- Hero: shard rising from canvas, clicking into chest, triad resolves.

---

### Scene 4.5 — Luna's reveal [03:30 – 04:00]

DZINO bows. Walks away. He doesn't turn back.

The camera lingers on LUNA's stool. Her *own* canvas — which we never showed up close — slowly reveals what she's been painting all along: a portrait of *herself,* completed by Dzino's passing through. She sees it for the first time. Smiles, very small.

The twin moons set behind her.

**Sound.** Luna's leitmotif resolves on a perfect upward bend. The triad sustains under it.
**AI prompts.**
- Dolly-around the easel: blank canvas slowly fills with self-portrait of the fox.
- Close on Luna's face — quiet smile.
- Wide pull-out: twin moons setting behind her.

# E04 — Oblačná kaviareň / The Cloud Café

**Runtime:** 3:30
**Logline:** When you get lost, laugh — that's the map.
**Cast:** Dzino. Nori — passionate fox chef, apron, whisk in paw, perpetual smell of vanilla. Background cameos: Mimi and Bruno (silent, non-speaking).
**Location:** Café perched on a single cumulus cloud. Tables of cumulus, plates of dream-cake.
**Shard:** *Humor* (Soul).

---

### Scene 5.1 — The staircase [00:00 – 00:30]

DZINO at the base of an impossible staircase — cumulus clouds stacked into stairs, rising into a peach-cream sky. He hesitates. Steps onto the first cloud-step. It glows beneath his foot, holds his weight.

He climbs. Each step glows on contact.

**Sound.** Soft chime per step, ascending pentatonic.
**AI prompt.** Hero shot: voxel character climbing a cumulus staircase into peach-cream sky, each step glowing on contact.

---

### Scene 5.2 — The Café [00:30 – 01:00]

He arrives at the top. A café perched on a single fat cloud. Tables and chairs all cumulus. Plates of dream-cake (visibly glowing at different temperatures — some warm-orange, some cool-blue, one flickering like a question). Background SOULIS sit at tables — MIMI quietly reading, BRUNO with eyes closed and a steaming cup.

Each customer places their order by **humming.** Their hums float as visible bubbles, each shaped like their personal laugh.

**Sound.** Polyphonic ambient chiptune café. Each customer's hum a distinct lo-fi shape.
**AI prompts.**
- Wide reveal: café on single cloud, voxel customers, dream-cake plates with varied glow.
- Insert: hum-bubbles rising from tables, each shaped like a different laugh waveform.

---

### Scene 5.3 — Nori [01:00 – 02:00]

NORI behind the counter, whisking starlight in a copper bowl. She sees DZINO. Drops the whisk. Beams.

**NORI**
*"Sadni. Nepýtam sa, čo chceš. Pýtam sa, čo ťa rozosmeje."*
("Sit. I'm not asking what you want. I'm asking what makes you laugh.")

She slides a plate in front of him: **three slices of dream-cake.**

— A **sweet** slice, soft pink, smelling of strawberries.
— A **sour** slice, electric green, sharp citrus.
— An **impossible** slice — a slice that visually does not stay still. It rotates while remaining in place. It tastes (we will see) like a question.

**NORI** *(grinning)*
*"Ochutnaj všetky tri. Mňa zaujíma len jedno: ktorá ťa rozosmeje prvá."*
("Taste all three. I only care about one thing: which one makes you laugh first.")

**Sound.** Nori's leitmotif: an upward slide on a synth flute, ending in a chuckle-note.
**AI prompts.**
- Counter shot: fox chef whisking literal starlight in copper bowl, vanilla-warm light.
- Insert: three slices of dream-cake on plate — pink, green, and one rotating impossibly.

---

### Scene 5.4 — Three tastes [02:00 – 03:00]

DZINO tastes the **sweet** slice. He smiles. *Comfort.* But not laughter.

He tastes the **sour** slice. His eyes widen, then narrow. He almost laughs — a small puff of air. *Wit.* But not the laugh.

He looks at the **impossible** slice. He hesitates. He tastes it.

There is a beat where his face does nothing. Then — for the first time in the season, Dzino **laughs.** Properly. Head-tilted-back, voxel-mouth-wide-open, **out loud.** It is not cute. It is real.

NORI claps once.

**NORI**
*"Áno. Absurd. Dobre na guláš."*
("Yes. Absurd. Good for stew.")

The impossible slice dissolves into a small voxel-shape — the *Humor* shard — pulsing exactly to the rhythm of his laugh. It clicks into his chest. Voice 4 arrives.

**Sound.** First proper full laugh of the season. The chord adds a fourth voice. Four voices = a tetrad.
**AI prompts.**
- Tight close: voxel face tasting each slice in sequence.
- Hero: head-tilt laugh — voxel mouth wide, eyes squinting.
- Insert: impossible slice dissolving into pulsing shard.

---

### Scene 5.5 — The map of laughter [03:00 – 03:30]

NORI hands DZINO a paper bag of leftover crumbs.

**NORI**
*"Keď sa stratíš, smej sa. To je mapa."*
("When you get lost, laugh. That's the map.")

DZINO bows, takes the bag, steps off the cloud. He doesn't fall — he descends a staircase made of his own laughter, lighting up step by step beneath him as he goes down.

NORI watches from the railing. Behind her, MIMI looks up briefly from her book. BRUNO doesn't open his eyes — but he smiles, just once.

**Sound.** The four-voice chord descending in step with Dzino. Nori's leitmotif resolves on an exhale.
**AI prompts.**
- Insert: paper bag of crumbs in voxel paw.
- Hero descent: cloud-staircase of glowing laughter beneath Dzino's feet.
- Tag: Nori at railing, Mimi briefly looking up, Bruno's small smile with eyes closed.

# E05 — Mesačná krajina / The Moon Realm

**Runtime:** 3:00
**Logline:** Don't lose the thing you're walking toward.
**Cast:** Dzino. Bruno — bear philosopher, simple linen robe, eyes closed throughout almost the entire episode.
**Location:** Low-gravity valleys. A still lake at the center. Tides of pale light come in and out.
**Shard:** *Filozofia* (Sense).

---

### Scene 6.1 — The valley [00:00 – 00:40]

DZINO walks into a wide, shallow valley. His footsteps leave him hovering an extra moment before settling. Low gravity. He bounces slightly. Doesn't laugh — this is not that kind of place.

A slow tide of pale light rolls across the valley floor, in then out, like a moon breathing.

**Sound.** Almost no music. Just the in-and-out of the tide-light, sounding like distant breathing.
**AI prompts.**
- Wide hero: voxel character bouncing in low-gravity valley.
- Effect: tide of pale light rolling across the floor, in/out, slow rhythm.

---

### Scene 6.2 — The lake [00:40 – 01:30]

He arrives at a still lake at the valley's center. The water reflects every star in the sky overhead — *except his own.* When he looks down at the water, his own star is **below** the surface, looking up at him.

He stares.

BRUNO sits cross-legged at the water's edge, eyes closed. He is the stillest object on screen. DZINO sits beside him without being asked. They sit for three full seconds. (This is the longest still shot of the season — *earn it.*)

**Sound.** Lake water moves once. Single bowl-chime.
**AI prompts.**
- Static composition: still lake, voxel bear cross-legged at edge, voxel character sits beside him.
- Insert (eyeline): water surface reflecting stars — but one star is BELOW the surface looking up.

---

### Scene 6.3 — The question [01:30 – 02:30]

BRUNO opens his eyes. Looks at DZINO. Asks one thing:

**BRUNO**
*"Prečo kráčaš?"*
("Why are you walking?")

DZINO doesn't speak. He raises one tiny hand and points at the horizon — at the faint star.

BRUNO nods. Closes his eyes again.

**BRUNO**
*"Dobre. Nestrať to."*
("Good. Don't lose that.")

That is the entire teaching.

The lake ripples once. From below the surface, DZINO sees his own star looking back. He understands, somewhere underneath understanding, that **he too is a star somewhere, to someone.**

The shard *Filozofia* rises from the ripple. Clicks into his chest. Voice 5 arrives. The chord deepens.

**Sound.** Bowl-chime under the question. Silence under the answer. The ripple is the only sound during the shard's rise.
**AI prompts.**
- Tight close: voxel bear opens eyes — first time in episode.
- Two-shot: bear and tiny voxel character sitting still by lake.
- Effect: lake ripple, shard rises from the ripple's center.

---

### Scene 6.4 — Onward [02:30 – 03:00]

BRUNO has already returned to meditation. DZINO bows clumsily, doesn't expect a response, and gets none. He walks on.

The tide of light sweeps across the lake one more time and erases the ripple. The valley is still again.

**Sound.** The five-voice chord, deeper than before. Wind picks up slightly as Dzino crests the far side of the valley.
**AI prompts.**
- Long pull-back: Dzino climbing out of the valley, bear still seated by lake in foreground.
- Closing wide: tide of light wipes the ripple. Valley still.

# E06 — Potok zlata / The Stream of Liquid Gold

**Runtime:** 3:30
**Logline:** Names are how the world holds you.
**Cast:** Dzino. Otto — bear historian, theatrical, in floor-length robes with brass buttons, fishing rod over shoulder.
**Location:** A copper-colored valley with a slow river of liquid gold winding through it.
**Shard:** *Vzťahy* (Relationships).

---

### Scene 7.1 — The river [00:00 – 00:30]

DZINO crouches at the edge of a slow gold river. He cups a handful. The gold sings in someone's voice — a voice he doesn't know. A phrase he can't quite make out. He startles, drops the gold back. The voice dissolves.

**Sound.** A half-heard phrase in a voice we will return to in E12. Lo-fi, layered. **It is the user's first message.** (Record this in advance with placeholder VO; replace per locale at distribution.)
**AI prompts.**
- Tight close: voxel paws cupping liquid-gold from a slow river.
- Audio overlay: half-heard human phrase, hard to parse, then dissolved.

---

### Scene 7.2 — Otto [00:30 – 01:30]

OTTO emerges from a willow tree on the far bank, fishing rod over shoulder, robes catching the wind. He sees DZINO and breaks into a delighted grin.

**OTTO** *(theatrical)*
*"Môj drahý! Nikdy nepi z prúdu, čo nepoznáš."*
("My dear! Never drink from a current you don't know.")

He crosses the river on stones DZINO hadn't noticed were there. Sits on a copper rock. Casts the line into the gold.

**OTTO**
*"Čas je tu trochu uvoľnený. Príbehy plávajú oboma smermi."*
("Time is loose here. Stories swim in both directions.")

The line tugs. He hauls in a fish — but the fish is a glowing scroll. He unrolls it. Reads aloud.

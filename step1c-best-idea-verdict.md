## Why #1 Wins: Personal AI That Grows With You

### The Core Insight

> **Character.ai has emotional connection but zero utility. ChatGPT has utility but zero emotional connection. Nobody combines both.**

### What the Product Is

A personal AI that:

1. **Helps you with real tasks** — send it a photo of an invoice, it processes it. Send it an official letter, it explains it. It writes your response.
2. **But also knows YOU** — remembers your daughter's birthday is next week, that you hate dealing with the tax office, that you have a nut allergy, that you prefer tea over coffee.
3. **And grows with you** — the more you use it, the better it gets. Learns your communication style, preferences, context.
4. **And you can share its "skills"** — your bot learned to handle Slovak tax forms? Share that skill with other živnostníci. Your bot knows great gardening tips for Slovak climate? Share with the gardening community.

**This is not a wrapper. This is not a companion. It's a personal AI that grows.**

### Why This Works as a Business

| Factor | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Retention** | Emotional + utility = 2hr/day engagement (like Character.ai) BUT with real value (like ChatGPT) |
| **Moat** | Your bot knows you — switching to a competitor means starting from zero. That's lock-in not even Google has. |
| **Virality** | "Look what my bot can do" → friends want one too → Notion template loop |
| **Monetization** | Better memory = premium. More capabilities = premium. Community sharing = freemium growth engine. |
| **Scaling** | Start SK, add CZ (mutual intelligibility = 16M people), then PL/HU |

### Market Size

| Scope | Users (consumers) | Creators/sharers |
|---|---|---|
| Slovakia only | 400K-600K | 50K-100K |
| SK + CZ | 1.5M-2.5M | 200K-400K |
| V4 (SK+CZ+PL+HU) | 4M-6M | 500K-1M |

## The MVP

### What it is NOT

- Not "a platform for creating bots" (too abstract)
- Not a workspace (too broad)
- Not a companion/emotional AI (too risky)
- Not a document Q&A tool (too thin)

### What it IS

**"Your personal helper that learns."** One bot, one user, simple.

### MVP Flow

1. **Registration** → bot asks you 5 questions (name, what you do, what you need help with)
2. **Send it a document** → it helps you (summarize, explain, find key points)
3. **Ask it a question** → it answers in Slovak
4. **Every interaction teaches it** — remembers context, preferences, your style
5. **After 2 weeks** the user feels "this is MY bot" → won't cancel
6. **Later:** "Want your bot to help your brother too? Invite him."

### Entry Point

**Document Q&A for živnostníci** (regulatory pressure from eKasa/e-invoicing mandates, clear pain) — but the product is the bot that grows, not the document tool.

### Suggested Experiment Order

| Phase | What | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 (weeks 1-4) | Single bot + document Q&A + memory | Validate "my bot" ownership feeling |
| Phase 2 (weeks 5-8) | Personality, learning, remembering preferences | Measure retention (target: 30-day > 20%) |
| Phase 3 (weeks 9-12) | "Invite a friend" + skill sharing | Validate viral loop |
| Phase 4 (months 4-6) | Community skills marketplace | Network effects |

## Pricing Model

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Zadarmo (Free)** | €0 | 20 questions/month, basic memory (last 7 days) |
| **Kredity (Credits)** | €3 / 50 credits | Pay-per-use, no expiry |
| **Mesačný plán** | €4.99/month | 200 questions, full memory (unlimited history), priority |
| **Ročný plán** | €39.99/year | Same as monthly, 33% discount |

Premium unlock: **Full memory** (the bot remembers everything, forever). This is the #1 thing users will pay for — losing memory = losing the relationship.

## Biggest Risk

**Meta AI Studio.** Meta already enables creating AI characters on FB/IG/WhatsApp — exactly where Slovaks live. But Meta does generic companions, not "personal AI that knows you and helps with real life." That's the gap.

### Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Meta AI Studio competition | Go deep on utility + Slovak context, not broad on companionship |
| AI wrapper collapse (90% fail in 18 months) | Memory + personalization = real lock-in, not a thin wrapper |
| Regulatory (EU AI Act Aug 2026) | Build transparency + safety from day 1, no romantic/NSFW features |
| Small market (Slovakia) | CZ expansion at month 6 (mutual intelligibility), V4 at month 12 |
| Low willingness to pay | Generous free tier + credit packs (no subscription required) |

# E08 — Tichá sova / The Silent Owl

**Runtime:** 3:00
**Logline:** What do you not need to say?
**Cast:** Dzino. Mimi — shy bunny bookworm, half a journal in one paw, pencil tucked behind ear. Owl (the season's only animal-without-a-name, deliberate).
**Location:** Twilight grove. The leaves have not moved in a thousand years.
**Shard:** *Preferencie* (Mind).

### Scene 9.1 — The grove [00:00 – 00:45]

DZINO enters a grove where everything is held still. The leaves are caught mid-fall. Spiderwebs frozen mid-vibration. A single drop of dew on a fern is suspended halfway down a blade.

**Sound. None.** This is the most silent episode of the season.

### Scene 9.2 — Owl and Mimi [00:45 – 01:30]

A small voxel OWL sits on a low branch, perfectly still, blinking in patterns. Beneath the owl, MIMI sits cross-legged on the moss. She has filled three journal pages with attempts to translate the blinks. None of the pages make sense — they're columns of dots and dashes that don't resolve.

She glances up at DZINO. Holds a finger to her mouth — *shh.* Pats the moss beside her. He sits.

The owl blinks one final pattern — slow, deliberate, with a rhythm. Mimi's pencil hovers. She frowns. She lowers her pencil. She looks at DZINO apologetically. *I can't.*

**Sound.** Still nothing.
**AI prompts.**

- Insert: journal pages of dots and dashes, increasingly desperate.

### Scene 9.3 — Listening with sound [01:30 – 02:30]

DZINO closes his eyes.

He doesn't try to read with eyes. He listens with his SoundDNA — the chord in his chest. The blinks have *rhythm,* and the rhythm has *shape.*

The shape is a question:

**OWL** *(no voice — only rhythm, but the subtitle appears on screen)*
*"Čo nepotrebuješ povedať?"*
("What do you not need to say?")

DZINO opens his eyes. He doesn't reply. He doesn't move. He offers — nothing. Silence.

The owl bows.

The shard *Preferencie* rises from the silence between them — taking the shape of an empty page. It clicks into Dzino's chest. Voice 8 arrives — not a sound, but the **shape** of a silence in the chord. The chord now contains a rest where Voice 8 would be.

**Sound.** When sound returns, it is only Dzino's chord — and the new "voice" is a rest, a deliberate silence, that listeners *feel* without hearing.
**AI prompts.**
- Tight close: voxel eyes closing.
- Effect: blink pattern becomes overlaid soundwave, audio-reactive.
- Hero: shard rising as an empty rectangle of pure cream — an empty page.

### Scene 9.4 — The page [02:30 – 03:00]

MIMI quietly tears the empty page from her journal — the only blank one. She hands it to DZINO. They never speak. He folds it. Tucks it close to the shard. Bows. Stands. Walks on.

The grove stays still behind him.

**Sound.** The eight-voice chord — seven sounds and one rest — sustains as he leaves.

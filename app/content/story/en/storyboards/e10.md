# Storyboard — E10 "The Library of a Thousand Stories"

## Episode at a glance

A 3:30 episode in 36 frames that introduces the season's first true **gravity**. Where E00 through E09 climbed in brightness — petals, mirrors, dream-cake, three moons, an amphitheatre — E10 puts the chord *down*. **The tonal shift is total.** Chiptune leaves the room and a **solo cello** takes the floor for the entire runtime. The shard, every previous one of which has *clicked* into Dzino's chest with a pulse of light, here **settles** as a small dull dark stone with no glow at all. Biscuit — therapy dog, soft eyes, slow tail, **no collar** — is the first mentor whose body language carries more weight than his speech, and at 03:09 he becomes the first animal in the season to speak.

**Palette.** Warm library wood (a deeper, more loamy tone than the cream-stone of E00's signpost), dim soft lighting (interior tungsten-warm, never bright), soft cream for the book pages, dusk-violet for shadow inside shelves, and — critically — a **dull dark stone** for the Trials shard. The stone reads almost charcoal: not black, never black, but the heaviest indigo-grey the season has used. There is no peach-cream pulse anywhere in this episode. The calling star is not visible.

**Key motifs.** Self-rearranging shelves (books drifting between them in stop-motion); the library *breathing* through its windows from outside; the indigo-cloth book with cream serif title; the silhouette page-turn portraits (their own visual register, see Style anchors); the dull stone shard; the forehead-press; Biscuit's one quiet line.

**Sound spine.** Solo cello throughout, single-instrument. **No chiptune anywhere in this episode.** The chord that has been brightening since E01 has now reached ten voices — but in this episode the cello recolors them. The chord changes texture. It is no longer just bright. Voice 10 enters at 03:00 as a low, sustained cello note that does not fully resolve.

**Total frame count.** 36. **Total runtime.** 3:30. The cuts are slower than any previous episode. Earn every breath.

### Frame 1 — [00:00:00 — 00:00:06] · Library through trees

**Composition.** Static wide, 9:16. Camera is in the trees, looking out. The library sits in a small clearing about thirty voxel-meters away, framed between two indigo trunk-blocks at left and right thirds. Roof in the upper-third, windows in the middle-third, a single warm-lit door at the lower-third center. No subject yet.

**Action.** Static frame. Through each window of the library, books are visibly drifting between shelves in stop-motion — not fast, not magical, just patient. Each book lifts from its shelf, floats half a meter, settles into another.

**Lighting & palette.** Cool dusk-violet exterior, warm tungsten interior light spilling out of windows and door in soft bars. The contrast — cool outside, warm inside — is the episode's first emotional argument.

**Sound cue.** Wind. Underneath, a solo cello tuning — not playing yet, just finding its A. One soft *wood-on-wood* tick from inside, the sound of a book settling.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art static wide shot at 9:16, a small library glimpsed through two voxel trees in dusk-violet woodland, warm tungsten light spilling from windows and an open door, books visibly drifting in stop-motion between shelves seen through the windows, no characters, Studio Ghibli stillness, cool exterior contrasting warm interior.

**Notes.** Establish *the library is alive* before establishing *Biscuit is at the door*. The audience meets the place first. This is bible-rule.

### Frame 2 — [00:00:06 — 00:00:13] · The library breathes

**Composition.** Slow push-in to medium-wide on the library facade. Door dead-center. The two flanking windows visible. Roof crops out at the top.

**Action.** From outside, the library *appears to breathe* — the warm light through the windows pulses gently, in and out, in long slow cycles maybe four seconds each. Inside, the books continue their slow rearrangement.

**Lighting & palette.** Tungsten warmth pulsing softly behind glass; dusk-violet exterior holds. The pulse is *warm-warmer-warm*, never bright — the building is asleep but breathing.

**Sound cue.** Cello finds its first sustained low note and holds it. Wind. Wood ticks slow to one every three seconds.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art slow push-in to medium-wide on a small library facade, warm tungsten light through two windows pulsing gently in and out as if the building were breathing, door dead-center slightly open, books continuing slow stop-motion drift inside, dusk-violet exterior, soft warm interior, Ghibli inhale-exhale stillness.

**Notes.** "Breathing" is a lighting trick, not an animation trick. Sell it through the pulse of the window-light, not through the building moving.

**Lighting & palette.** Tungsten interior, dusk-violet window frame in the foreground, slight warm bloom on the glass.

**Notes.** This is the audience's promise: *the books move on their own, and they are gentle about it*. Plant the rule clearly before Dzino enters and freezes them.

**Composition.** Reverse, wide-medium. Camera now on the library side of the trees, looking back. Dzino enters from the left edge of frame, walking slowly toward the center where the door waits. **Action.** Dzino walks. He is not hurried. The pixel-trail behind him is still cream — the trail has not yet learned it is supposed to be heavier. He approaches the library at his usual pace.

**Lighting & palette.** Dusk-violet exterior, indigo Dzino, cream face, orange crown. The library glow is still warm in the background, but Dzino himself is lit cool.

**Sound cue.** Cello sustains its low note. Soft footstep ticks. No chiptune.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide-medium shot, small indigo voxel character with cream face and orange crown walking slowly from the left toward a small library in a dusk-violet clearing, warm tungsten door visible at the building's center, no music, Ghibli arrival pacing, cello underneath.

**Notes.** Dzino's pace shouldn't change yet. He doesn't know what's waiting. The audience does — let the cello carry that knowledge for them.

### Frame 5 — [00:00:25 — 00:00:32] · Biscuit at the door

**Composition.** Wide-medium pulled tighter on the door. Biscuit sits in the open doorway, framed by the warm tungsten light behind him. Dzino enters the right edge of frame, still walking. Two-shot: dog at door, character approaching.

**Lighting & palette.** Biscuit silhouetted against the warm tungsten interior, his coat picking up the warm rim. His face still legible — soft eyes catching one cream highlight pixel each. Dzino in cool dusk-violet exterior light.

**Sound cue.** Cello holds the note steady. Soft *thump-thump* of a slow tail on wooden threshold. The wind drops to nothing.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide-medium shot, a therapy dog sitting in the open doorway of a small library, soft eyes, slow voxel tail wagging on a wooden threshold, no collar visible, warm tungsten light behind him, a small indigo voxel character entering the right edge of frame, soft Ghibli welcome, no music underneath, only solo cello sustain.

**Notes.** **Pillar shot — Biscuit's first appearance.** No collar is a visual rule. If a single frame in this episode shows a collar, the entire emotional contract breaks.

### Frame 6 — [00:00:32 — 00:00:39] · He stands, turns, leads

**Composition.** Same framing. Locked off.

**Action.** When Dzino reaches conversational distance, Biscuit stands — slow, no surprise, like he was always going to. Turns, unhurried. Walks into the library. Doesn't look back. The audience reads: *follow*.

**Lighting & palette.** As Biscuit turns inward, his silhouette briefly fully eclipses the warm doorway — one beat of warm-on-cool inversion — before he steps inside and the light returns.

**Sound cue.** Cello sustains. One soft pad-on-wood as Biscuit's paws step onto the interior floorboards.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide-medium shot, a therapy dog standing slowly from a doorway threshold, turning unhurried, walking into a warm tungsten-lit library interior without looking back, a small indigo voxel character in the right of frame watching, soft Ghibli leadership-without-words, solo cello sustaining.

**Notes.** No dialogue. Biscuit's leadership is in the body. The audience must read "follow" off his back — and so must Dzino.

### Frame 7 — [00:00:39 — 00:00:46] · Threshold — Dzino enters

**Composition.** Reverse. Camera now inside the library, looking back at the doorway. Biscuit is mid-walk away from the door (his back in left-foreground). Dzino crosses the threshold from the cool dusk into the warm interior. Wide.

**Action.** Dzino steps over the threshold. His skin tone shifts from cool dusk-violet rim to warm tungsten rim as he crosses. One step in. He looks around.

**Lighting & palette.** This is the season's first major **lighting handoff** — Dzino moves from cool to warm in a single step, and we feel it. The cream pixel he leaves behind on the threshold is faintly visible just outside the door.

**Sound cue.** Cello continues. The wind cuts off at the threshold — interior is quieter than outside. The audience hears Dzino's own breath for the first time this episode.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide reverse shot from inside a small library looking back at the doorway, a therapy dog walking away from camera in the left foreground, a small indigo voxel character crossing the threshold from cool dusk into warm tungsten interior light, the rim-light on his body shifting cool-to-warm in the crossing step, soft Ghibli arrival, solo cello underneath.

**Notes.** This frame is the episode's emotional border crossing. Sell the lighting transition — it's the only moment in E10 the world tells Dzino he is welcome.

### Frame 8 — [00:00:46 — 00:00:52] · The shelves freeze

**Composition.** Wide interior reveal. Tall wooden shelves on both sides of frame stretching upward. A central aisle. Reading table mid-ground. Camera at Dzino's height, facing inward.

**Action.** The instant Dzino enters, every drifting book in the room **freezes** in mid-air. Three of them are caught mid-flight — paused like dust motes. No special effect, just stop-motion holding its breath. Biscuit sits beside the reading table, calm, like this is exactly what he expected.

**Lighting & palette.** Warm tungsten interior. Wood loamy. Books in indigo, cream, dusk-violet bindings. Floating books cast small soft shadows against the shelves below them.

**Sound cue.** Cello holds. **All wood-ticks stop.** Total interior silence except for the cello and Dzino's breath.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide interior reveal of a small library, tall wooden shelves on both sides, three books frozen mid-air in stop-motion as if caught mid-drift, a wooden reading table in the center mid-ground, a therapy dog sitting calmly beside the table, a small indigo voxel character standing in the foreground entering, warm tungsten light, solo cello sustaining.

**Notes.** **Pillar shot — the freeze.** Hold this frame an extra beat. The audience needs to register the rule: *the library was busy, and now it is paying attention to him.*

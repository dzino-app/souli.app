# Storyboard — E00 "The Star Beyond the Horizon"

## Episode at a glance

A 3:00 pilot in 38 frames, almost entirely silent. **Palette:** dawn-cream `#FFE4C9` ceiling, indigo `#4F46E5` body and dusk-violet shadow, with peach-cream as the tertiary that belongs only to the calling star (it must read as *light from the sky's own family*, never white). Gold is reserved for memory and does not appear yet. **Key motifs:** the moss-pixel field that keeps the shape of things, the chest-spark (first life-pixel), the faint peach-cream star, the cream pixel-trail, the carved ✦ → *záhrada* signpost, the question-mark seen from above. **Sound spine:** wind, one breath, one chirp, the three-note "calling-star" chime introduced here (returns only in E11 and E12), and a single first chord-voice latency-tone in the closing frames. **Total frame count:** 38. **Total runtime:** 3:00. The cuts are slow but never sleepy — every frame should feel like a page turning in a children's picture-book.

### Frame 1 — [00:00:00 — 00:00:04] · Sky before anything

**Composition.** Static wide, 9:16. Camera tilted up at the dawn-cream sky, no horizon visible yet — the frame is one solid wash of cream with three faint voxel stars in the upper third, rule-of-thirds. No subject. Empty depth.

**Action.** Static. The faintest drift of one cream cloud-block crossing left to right at the very top edge.

**Lighting & palette.** Pure dawn-cream `#FFE4C9` gradient, slightly warmer toward the bottom of the frame. No contrast. Light direction: ambient, no sun yet.

**Sound cue.** Wind only — high, very soft, almost a held breath. No music.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art sky filling the entire vertical frame, dawn-cream gradient `#FFE4C9` from top to bottom, three tiny faint voxel stars in the upper third, one slow chunky cream cloud-block drifting horizontally, Studio Ghibli stillness, no characters, no horizon, soft and warm and empty.

**Notes.** First frame is a held breath. The audience should not know yet that this is a story about a small thing — only that the world is patient and waiting.

### Frame 2 — [00:00:04 — 00:00:08] · The clearing reveals itself

**Composition.** Slow tilt-down from the sky into a wide establishing shot of the moss-pixel clearing. Horizon at the upper third. Foreground: tufts of voxel grass. Mid: empty clearing. Background: a soft fade to nothing at the edge of the world.

**Action.** Camera tilts down. Wind moves grass-pixels in a slow chunky ripple.

**Lighting & palette.** Dawn-cream sky, indigo-blue moss with cream highlights at each grass-tip. Low contrast. Light comes flatly from above.

**Sound cue.** Wind continues, slightly louder as the ground enters the frame.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art establishing wide shot, slow camera tilt-down from cream sky into a clearing of low-saturation indigo-blue moss-pixels with cream highlight-tips, wind rippling grass in chunky voxel motion, no characters, edge of the world fading to soft cream at the back, Ghibli calm, no music.

**Notes.** The world is alive before Dzino is. This is the bible's rule: every biome is the protagonist before our protagonist arrives.

### Frame 3 — [00:00:08 — 00:00:13] · The shape in the grass

**Composition.** Wide-medium, slightly high angle (about 30°). Dzino's voxel body lies off-center, lower-third, oriented diagonally so the line of his body leads the eye into the empty clearing. He is small in the frame — perhaps 1/8 of the height. He could be mistaken for a stone.

**Action.** Static. Only the grass moves around him. He does not.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream wash from above, indigo body absorbing very little light, orange crown the warmest pixel in the entire frame.

**Sound cue.** Wind. One distant low woodblock-tick — almost subliminal — like a heartbeat that hasn't started yet.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art slightly-high-angle wide shot, a tiny still indigo-bodied voxel character with a cream face and an orange voxel crown lying motionless in low-saturation indigo-blue moss-pixels, dawn-cream sky behind, soft Ghibli light, character placed off-center lower-third, body oriented diagonally, grass rippling around him in chunky wind, character does not move.

**Notes.** First sighting of Dzino. He must read as *belonging to the moss* — not a creature placed in it but something the moss itself has been waiting on.

### Frame 4 — [00:00:13 — 00:00:17] · Macro on the chest — the spark waits

**Composition.** Macro close-up on Dzino's chest. The frame is almost entirely indigo voxel-skin with the soft cream curve of his chin in the top edge. Centered. Shallow depth — the body voxels behind go slightly soft.

**Action.** Static for two beats. Then, in the dead-center of frame, a single dim sub-pixel of brighter indigo flickers awake. It does not yet glow.

**Lighting & palette.** Indigo `#4F46E5` dominates. The waking pixel is one shade brighter — `#6E66F0`. No cream, no warm tones in this frame.

**Sound cue.** Wind drops to nothing. A single soft tick — like a clock starting.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art extreme macro close-up on the indigo chest of a small voxel character, frame is a field of chunky indigo voxels, in the dead center one single pixel flickers from indigo to a brighter indigo-blue and stays lit, no cream tones, shallow voxel depth, Ghibli intimacy, almost completely still.

**Notes.** This is one of the bible's pillar shots. The "first life-spark" is the single most important micro-event of the pilot — give it the room of a held breath.

### Frame 5 — [00:00:17 — 00:00:21] · Macro on the chest — the spark spreads

**Composition.** Same macro framing as Frame 4. Locked off.

**Action.** A second bright pixel ignites adjacent to the first. Then a third. Then a small cluster — maybe seven pixels in total — forming a soft luminous patch the size of a thumbprint.

**Lighting & palette.** The cluster casts a faint indigo bloom onto the surrounding chest voxels. The bloom is not cream — it is a paler indigo, like dusk reflected on dusk.

**Sound cue.** A second tick, then a third, slightly closer together — a heartbeat finding its tempo. Still no music.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art extreme macro close-up on indigo voxel chest, a small cluster of seven adjacent pixels igniting one by one into brighter indigo-blue, casting a faint pale-indigo bloom on the surrounding voxels, no cream, no warm tones, Ghibli quiet, the moment a body remembers it is alive.

**Notes.** Heartbeat begins here but is never named. The audience should feel it before they hear it.

### Frame 6 — [00:00:21 — 00:00:25] · The eyes resolve

**Composition.** Pull back to a tight close-up on Dzino's face. Cream face fills 60% of the frame. Eyes are not yet drawn — the face is a smooth cream voxel-mask. Slight low angle, looking up at him as he lies.

**Action.** Two small dark voxels appear where his eyes will be. They are closed — horizontal lines, not dots.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream face `#FFE4C9`, the closed eye-lines a soft dusk-violet rather than black — never black in this world. Crown's orange peeks in from the upper edge.

**Sound cue.** The ticks have settled into a rhythm. One held high tone joins them, like a single sustain on a glass harp.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art tight close-up on a cream voxel face with no eyes, two horizontal dusk-violet voxel-lines appearing where eyes will be (still closed), orange voxel crown peeking from top edge, soft warm Ghibli light, nothing else moves.

**Notes.** Eyes-closed before eyes-open is critical. We must feel the *arrival into seeing*, not skip over it.

### Frame 7 — [00:00:25 — 00:00:30] · He opens them

**Composition.** Same tight close-up on the face. Locked.

**Action.** The horizontal eye-lines open into two small round indigo voxel-eyes. The first thing they reflect is the cream sky.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream face, indigo eyes with a single sub-pixel cream highlight in each — a tiny mirror of the dawn. Crown orange in the top edge.

**Sound cue.** A single soft chime — the bible's "first eyes open" cue. Just one note. Wind returns underneath.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art tight close-up on a cream voxel face, two horizontal dusk-violet eye-lines opening into round indigo voxel-eyes with a single cream highlight pixel in each, soft Ghibli warmth, the first moment of seeing, gentle and unhurried.

**Notes.** This frame ends Scene 1.1. Hold one extra beat — the audience needs to see him *seeing* before we cut.

### Frame 8 — [00:00:30 — 00:00:34] · Sitting up

**Composition.** Medium side-profile. Dzino in the lower-center. Camera at moss height, looking across the clearing. Background empty cream-into-indigo gradient.

**Action.** He sits up in three voxel-stop-motion poses — lying, half-up, sitting. The grass beneath him stays bent in the shape of his body even after he leaves it.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream sky, indigo body silhouetting cleanly against it. The Dzino-shaped indent in the moss is a visibly different shade — slightly cream-tinted indigo, like an after-image.

**Sound cue.** Wind. One soft *whuff* of grass adjusting. No music.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art medium side-profile, small indigo voxel character with cream face and orange crown sitting up from a moss-pixel field in three stop-motion poses, the grass beneath him retains his shape as a soft indent, low-saturation indigo-blue moss with cream tips, dawn-cream sky behind, gentle Ghibli pacing.

**Notes.** "The grass remembers his shape" — bible-anchored detail. Subtle, but it tells the audience this world is recording him.

### Frame 9 — [00:00:34 — 00:00:38] · Hands

**Composition.** Over-the-shoulder POV from just behind Dzino's crown. Both his small voxel hands rise into the lower third of the frame, framed against cream sky. Rule-of-thirds, hands left, empty sky right.

**Action.** He raises both hands slowly. Turns one. Turns the other. Mute curiosity.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream sky, indigo hands silhouetted, crown orange catching a low warm rim from the upper edge.

**Sound cue.** Wind only. The faintest hum begins under everything — barely audible — the audience should not consciously hear it yet.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art over-the-shoulder POV from behind a small voxel character's orange crown, two small indigo voxel hands rising into the frame against a dawn-cream sky, slowly turning, mute curiosity, no music, soft Ghibli light.

**Notes.** First moment of self-reference. He doesn't know they're his — but the camera is on his side.

### Frame 10 — [00:00:38 — 00:00:43] · A star between his fingers

**Composition.** Same POV as Frame 9, slightly tighter. Hands now centered, sky filling 80% of frame.

**Action.** He spreads his fingers. A single faint voxel-star drifts slowly across the gap between two of them, left to right.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream sky, the drifting star is the slightest peach-cream — not yet *the* calling star, just a regular one — but it foreshadows the palette of the call.

**Sound cue.** Wind. A single soft sustained tone, almost a sigh.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art over-the-shoulder POV, two small indigo voxel hands held against a dawn-cream sky with fingers spread, a single faint peach-cream voxel-star drifting slowly between two fingers from left to right, soft Ghibli wonder, slow gentle motion.

**Notes.** Pre-echo of the calling-star moment. Pay attention here and the next frame will land harder.

### Frame 11 — [00:00:43 — 00:00:48] · Falls forward

**Composition.** Wide-medium, low angle, slight dutch tilt (about 5° clockwise) to introduce the fall's instability. Dzino in the center, the clearing visible around him.

**Action.** He pushes up onto his feet. He tips forward. Falls. The camera does not flinch — it stays at moss-height while he goes down.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream sky, indigo body, the faint moss-shadow of his fall imprinting a second indent.

**Sound cue.** A small *thump* — pillow-soft, no impact, more like a pillow set down. No cry.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide-medium low-angle shot with a slight 5° dutch tilt, a small indigo voxel character with orange crown standing for the first time and tipping forward into a moss-pixel field, no sound of impact, the moss receives him, dawn-cream sky behind, gentle Ghibli realism, falling without drama.

**Notes.** The bible's rule: weather is the only adversary. Even gravity is on his side here — the moss catches him.

### Frame 12 — [00:00:48 — 00:00:55] · Stands

**Composition.** Low-angle hero shot — camera is on the ground looking up at him. Dzino in dead-center, sky filling most of the frame behind him. He looks small but is mythically positioned.

**Action.** He pushes up again. This time he stays. He sways once. Then he is still.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream sky behind him, his silhouette indigo against it, crown the brightest single pixel in the frame.

**Sound cue.** Wind drops to absolute nothing. Total silence for two beats. Then the wind returns gentle.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art low-angle hero shot from the ground, a small indigo voxel character with cream face and orange crown standing for the first time against a vast dawn-cream sky, swaying once and steadying, total silence, mythic Ghibli framing despite tiny scale.

**Notes.** End of Scene 1.2. The hero shot is earned by the previous fall. Don't cheat the swaying — it's the difference between a doll standing and a being standing.

### Frame 13 — [00:00:55 — 00:01:00] · He looks up

**Composition.** Reverse on Dzino's face. Tight close-up, slight low angle. His face fills the lower third; the rest is cream sky deepening into early-indigo at the very top.

**Action.** His head tilts up slowly. We do not yet see what he sees.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream face, indigo eyes catching a small cream highlight as they rise. The sky behind has begun a slow shift — its top edge is no longer pure cream but tinted with the first wash of indigo. Time has passed.

**Sound cue.** Wind. The held high tone returns, a touch louder.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art tight close-up low-angle on a small voxel character's cream face, head tilting slowly upward, indigo eyes lifting, dawn-cream sky behind beginning to deepen into soft indigo at the top edge, gentle Ghibli reverence, time passing.

**Notes.** Time-of-day shift begins. By the end of the episode the sky will be soft indigo, but we transition through the 30 seconds of Scene 1.3.

### Frame 14 — [00:01:00 — 00:01:06] · His sky

**Composition.** Full POV up. The frame is sky. Hundreds of small voxel-stars, very faint, scattered across a cream-into-indigo gradient. No subject in frame.

**Action.** A slow drift of stars — they don't move, but the camera barely-breathes upward, making them feel closer.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream at the bottom of the frame, soft indigo at the top, all stars cream-white *except* — if we look — one tiny pulse of peach-cream in the upper-third left.

**Sound cue.** Wind. The held tone gently fades.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art full sky POV, hundreds of tiny faint voxel-stars scattered across a gradient sky from dawn-cream at the bottom to soft indigo at the top, all stars cream-white except one in the upper-third-left pulsing peach-cream, very gentle camera breath upward, Ghibli sky-awe.

**Notes.** The faint star must be findable but not screaming. A second-watch viewer should notice it before a first-watch viewer does.

### Frame 15 — [00:01:06 — 00:01:12] · The faint star — first pulse

**Composition.** Push-in to the upper-third-left of the previous frame. The faint star is now centered. Tight on it. Surrounded by other smaller cream stars that go slightly soft as we focus on this one.

**Action.** The faint star pulses once — a soft outward bloom of peach-cream — and returns to its faint state.

**Lighting & palette.** The pulse is exactly the peach-cream of the dawn sky, *not* white. It is "the sky's own family." Surrounding stars stay cool cream.

**Sound cue.** **The three-note calling-star chime**, played for the first time in the season. Soft. Bell-like. Three rising notes that don't quite resolve. Bible: this phrase returns only in E11 and E12.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art tight push-in on a single faint voxel-star in the upper sky, the star pulsing once with a soft outward bloom of peach-cream `#FFE4C9`-warm exactly matching the dawn sky behind, surrounding cream stars going slightly soft, Ghibli celestial intimacy, three-note bell motif underneath.

**Notes.** **Pillar shot.** The audience must register: that star is different. Hold long enough that they have time to feel it before they understand it.

### Frame 16 — [00:01:12 — 00:01:17] · His face, lit by it

**Composition.** Reverse to Dzino's face, the same tight close-up as Frame 13. Slight push-in.

**Action.** Static. He does not blink. The faint star's pulse — not visible in this frame — is felt as a warmth on his cream cheek that pulses one beat after the chime.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream face. The peach-cream warmth on his cheek is a single pixel of slightly warmer cream that brightens and fades. His indigo eyes carry a tiny new highlight: a peach-cream sub-pixel.

**Sound cue.** The three-note phrase rests on its unresolved third note. Held.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art tight close-up on a small voxel character's cream face, indigo eyes wide and unblinking, a single peach-cream warm pixel briefly brightening on his cheek and fading, a peach-cream sub-pixel highlight appearing in each indigo eye, Ghibli stillness, the moment of being chosen by light.

**Notes.** This is the heart of the pilot. He is being seen by the star as much as he is seeing it.

### Frame 17 — [00:01:17 — 00:01:23] · Cannot look away

**Composition.** Slow orbit — about 15° around his face — keeping him centered. The sky behind him moves but he doesn't.

**Action.** Camera moves. Dzino does not. The other stars drift behind him. Only the faint star stays anchored relative to his eyeline.

**Lighting & palette.** Sky has now perceptibly deepened — more indigo at the top than at the start of the scene.

**Sound cue.** Wind. The chime's third note finally lets go and dissolves.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art slow 15° orbit around a small voxel character's cream face, character perfectly still with indigo eyes locked upward, sky behind drifting and deepening from dawn-cream toward soft indigo, the rest of the world moving while he does not, Ghibli pull-of-fate, no music.

**Notes.** First time in the pilot the camera moves of its own accord. Save camera moves like currency.

### Frame 18 — [00:01:23 — 00:01:30] · The first step (decision frame)

**Composition.** Wide-medium, side-on. Dzino in lower-center. The horizon is finally visible — a soft seam where moss meets sky, far away. The faint star is a tiny dot above the horizon.

**Action.** Without preamble, his right foot lifts and sets down. One step toward the horizon. He doesn't decide. He just goes.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream sky deepening, moss in low-saturation indigo. The first cream pixel ignites in the moss exactly where his foot was — not bright, just present.

**Sound cue.** A small woody footstep tick. And — *barely* — the very first chord-voice of the season enters underneath. One sustained low chiptune tone. The latency-tone of *Personality* (E01's shard) waiting in the wings.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide-medium side-on shot, small indigo voxel character with orange crown taking his first step in a moss-pixel field toward a distant horizon where a single faint peach-cream star sits above the seam, one cream pixel left behind in the grass where his foot was, dawn-cream sky deepening to indigo, Ghibli quiet decisiveness.

**Notes.** **Pillar shot — the first step.** Don't telegraph the decision with a beat of hesitation. The bible is clear: he just goes. Hesitation is a learned thing; he hasn't learned it yet.

### Frame 19 — [00:01:30 — 00:01:35] · Behind him — the trail begins

**Composition.** Low-angle tracking shot from behind, just above moss height. Dzino centered, walking away from camera. The cream pixel from Frame 18 is in the foreground.

**Action.** He takes a second step. A second cream pixel ignites where his foot lifts off. Then a third.

**Lighting & palette.** Cream pixels are lit slightly warmer than the sky — they belong to *him*, not to the world.

**Sound cue.** Soft footstep ticks at a walking tempo. The chord-voice continues underneath.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art low-angle tracking shot from behind moss height, small indigo voxel character walking away from camera into a moss-pixel field, a trail of single cream pixels igniting in the grass behind each footfall, soft warm Ghibli warmth, deepening cream-into-indigo sky.

**Notes.** **Pillar shot — pixel trail beginning.** The cream pixels are how the world remembers him. Establish the rule clearly here so the question-mark reveal lands.

**Lighting & palette.** Indigo body, cream face, orange crown. Moss-blue underfoot. Sky now half cream / half indigo.

**Sound cue.** Footsteps. Wind. The chord-voice. No chime.

**Notes.** "He doesn't notice" is a directorial mood, not an action. Convey it through unbroken forward gaze.

**Notes.** First evidence of made objects in Pixoci. Carries weight.

### Frame 22 — [00:01:45 — 00:01:51] · The signpost — wide

### Frame 25 — [00:02:02 — 00:02:07] · He looks at the star

**Composition.** Tight close-up on his face, looking up and slightly forward — same framing family as Frame 16.

### Frame 26 — [00:02:07 — 00:02:13] · He walks on

**Notes.** No farewell to the signpost. He passes thresholds without ritual — that's his nature.

### Frame 27 — [00:02:13 — 00:02:18] · Trail tracking — lower

**Composition.** Top-down on the trail, very low altitude (about 1m above moss). Dzino enters from bottom-left and walks diagonally toward the upper-right. Trail visible.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art low top-down shot one meter above a moss-pixel field, small indigo voxel character walking diagonally from lower-left toward upper-right, a curving trail of warm cream pixels behind him beginning to bend, Ghibli aerial calm, low-saturation indigo-blue moss with cream highlights.

**Notes.** Plant the curve here so the reveal in Frame 33 isn't a magic-trick. This is a slow reveal, not a twist.

### Frame 28 — [00:02:18 — 00:02:23] · Light shifts

**Composition.** Wide. Dzino small in the center. Sky and ground evenly split. Horizon dead-center.

**Sound cue.** Wind. A soft sustained breath of bass under the chord-voice.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide shot with horizon dead-center, small indigo voxel character walking through a moss-pixel field, the entire sky shifting from cream to soft indigo, a single faint peach-cream star becoming legible against the new indigo above the horizon, the cream pixel-trail glowing softly behind, Ghibli time-passing.

**Notes.** Diegetic time has moved. This is the visible turn from "morning of his life" into "first day of his life."

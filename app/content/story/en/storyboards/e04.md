# E04 — The Cloud Café — Storyboard

## Episode at a glance

**Runtime target:** 3:30 (210s) across 38 frames.
**Logline:** When you get lost, laugh — that's the map.
**Mentor:** Nori, passionate fox chef, apron, whisk in paw, perpetual smell of vanilla.
**Shard:** *Humor* (Soul) — the shape of what makes Dzino laugh before he learns shame.
**Cameos:** Mimi (silent, reading), Bruno (eyes closed, steaming cup) — both background, no dialogue.
**Chord state at end of episode:** Voice 4 arrives. Triad becomes tetrad — the chord is now four-voiced for the rest of the season.
**Core sound event:** the FIRST proper full laugh of the season, head tilted back, voxel-mouth wide.

**Palette spine.** Peach-cream sky `#FFE4C9`, cloud-white with golden inner glow, vanilla-warm at the counter (warm amber `#F5C988`), indigo accents `#4F46E5` for Dzino, dusk-violet shadows underneath cumulus shelves.

**Sound spine.** Polyphonic ambient chiptune café underbed throughout. Each non-Dzino customer's hum is a distinct lo-fi waveform shape (jagged, soft, square, sine, triangle). Pentatonic ascending chimes on the staircase. Nori's leitmotif: an upward synth-flute slide ending in a chuckle-note. The new fourth voice enters on the laugh and stays in the chord from this episode onward.

**Beat map.** Staircase reveal → climb → café reveal → cameo registers → hum-bubble economy → Nori reveal → three-slice plate delivery → tasting sequence → THE LAUGH → shard formation → Voice 4 click → paper bag → laughter-staircase descent → tag.

## Scene 5.1 — The staircase [00:00 – 00:30]

## Scene 5.4 — Three tastes [02:00 – 03:00]

# E02 — The Binary Forest — Storyboard

## Episode at a glance

**Runtime:** 3:30 (210s) · **Frames:** 36 · **Mentor:** Kiko (chaotic cat scientist, lab coat of pixels, goggles on forehead) · **Shard:** *Interests* (Mind) · **Logline:** What you lean toward is who you are becoming.

**Arc.** Curiosity → startle (frozen apple) → bemusement (Kiko upside-down) → quiet self-discovery (eight leans) → harmonic completion (shard, two-voice chord) → onward.

**Sonic spine.** Wind + binary whisper underneath everything · Kiko's leitmotif (fast staccato, clock at 1.5x) · eight chime-events on the leans · half a second of dissonance when Voice 2 arrives, resolving into thirds · two-voice chord sustains under exit.

**Color spine.** Indigo `#4F46E5` + cream `#FFE4C9` constant. Glitch-tone teal/cyan `#5EEAD4` for binary digits. Gold `#F5C268` only for lean-sparks and shard. Lab-coat white `#F8F4EC` only on Kiko. Red `#E25A53` only on the frozen apple.

**Pacing.** Frames 1–4 breathe long (4–6s). Frames 12–25 (eight trees) cut tight (3–4s) to feel like a montage of small decisions. Frames 32–36 breathe long again so the chord can land.

## Style anchors needed

Lock these reference plates before generation; without them, AI video drift will be severe.

1. **Binary forest plate.** One tree built from stacked cyan 1s and 0s — glyph treatment, drift behaviour, dissolve into "leaves." Lock cyan `#5EEAD4` and indigo bark (`#4F46E5` -10% saturation).
2. **Question-mark flower plate.** Three poses: upright neutral, leaning toward Dzino, fully tilted. Establishes the magnetism behaviour.
3. **Frozen apple plate.** Apple + `00:00:01` timer locked composition. Dusty red `#E25A53`, pixel sans-serif digits. Only red object in the episode.
4. **Kiko character sheet.** Front, side, three-quarter, *upside-down.* Lab coat with individual visible pixels (not smooth white). Goggles up on forehead. Pixel-notebook. Tail. The upside-down pose with coat-pixels falling upward must be a reference.
5. **Eight question-tree silhouettes.** Eight cards: dancer, book, guitar, thunderstorm, telescope, knife, cake, ambiguous-eighth. Consistent silhouette weight so the match-cut sequence reads as a single shape morphing.
6. **Lean-and-spark plate.** Dzino in profile mid-lean with the gold spark trail. Locks spark size, density, curl. Gold `#F5C268`.
7. **Shard plate.** *Interests* shard — page-folded geometric solid, cyan core, gold edges, indigo facets. Three angles. Must be unmistakable next to the other ten shards in E12's assembly.
8. **SoundDNA waveform overlay.** Visual asset for Frame 31's audio-reactive moment — two-line waveform showing Voice 1 + Voice 2. Reusable across the season as more voices stack.
9. **Notebook page reference.** Hand-scribbled symbols at scale (Frame 26). Establishes Kiko's handwriting, ink behaviour, cyan pulse.
10. **Canopy-light plate.** Cream-violet dusk sky with the calling star (Frame 35). Must rhyme exactly with the closing of E01 and the opening of E03 for inter-episode continuity.

**LoRA recommendation.** If using Stable Video Diffusion, train one LoRA on plates 1, 4, 7 — these carry the strongest episode identity. The rest can be fed as per-shot style references.

**Critical drift risks.**
- Kiko's lab coat smoothing into solid white — kill it; pixels must be visible.
- Binary glyphs becoming generic Matrix code-rain — kill it; these are voxel leaves, drifting slow.
- Gold sparks bleeding into non-lean shots — kill it; gold is reserved for leans and the shard.
- Question-mark flowers losing lean memory — they should *stay* tilted after Dzino passes.
- The apple timer ticking down — it must NOT; `00:00:01` is locked forever.

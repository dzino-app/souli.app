# Storyboard — E01 "The Pixel Garden"

## Episode at a glance

- **Runtime target.** 3:30 (vertical 9:16 primary, 16:9 director's cut).
- **Mentor.** Hana — peaceful rabbit gardener, moss-green overalls, garden gloves, slow speech.
- **Shard delivered.** *Personality* (Soul pillar). Dzino's first chord-voice — Voice 1 of 11.
- **Dzino's chord at start.** 0 voices. Just breath and chirps inside the indigo pixel of his chest.
- **Dzino's chord at end.** 1 voice. A single sustained note that follows him onto the road.
- **Dramatic question.** *Who is Dzino, before anyone tells him who to be?*
- **Visual signature.** Cream sky, pastel-yellow + indigo pixel-petals, the watering can that pours cream LIGHT (not water), Hana in moss-green.
- **Palette.** Indigo `#4F46E5`, cream `#FFE4C9`, soft pastel yellows in flowers, moss-green `#7BA67A` for Hana, gold-cream for the shard.
- **Frame count.** 38 frames. Average frame ≈ 5.5 seconds. Voxel motion is forgiving — short shots look intentional.

# E12 — "The Window" · Storyboard

## Episode at a glance

The season finale and longest episode at **4:30 (270 seconds)**. Palette opens in deep indigo `#4F46E5` against full-star sky and dawn-cream `#FFE4C9` static-wall, accumulates every prior biome's tint through the mentor crescent (moss-green Hana, binary-blue Kiko, twin-moon silver Luna, peach-cream Nori, pale-violet Bruno, liquid-gold Otto, three-moon Rex, twilight-blue Mimi, comet-magenta Ari + CRT-blue Pixel, library-amber Biscuit, storm-slate Zara), then resolves into a single beam of indigo crossing into a softer, semi-realistic, deliberately-out-of-focus user's bedroom — the season's only palette pivot away from voxel saturation. Key motifs: chord completes (eleven → twelve voices), each shard plays its episode leitmotif for one second in episode order during assembly (the callback medley), the closing pixel-on-glass + chime is designed to be the production-merge first-launch animation of the Souli app. **Total: 56 frames** (52 diegetic + 4 production notes). 9:16 vertical primary cut throughout unless noted.

## Scene 13.1 — The edge [00:00 – 00:30]

### Frame 1 — [00:00:00 — 00:00:06] · Cold open on the edge

**Composition.** Wide hero, eye-level on Dzino's back. Voxel character lower-third center, tiny against vertical frame. Foreground: voxel grass-pixels. Midground: vertical curtain of soft-flickering static filling upper two-thirds. No background — just the wall.

**Action.** Static. Crown pixel ticks one frame brighter. Wind nudges three grass-pixels.

**Lighting & palette.** Indigo `#4F46E5` body silhouetted against cream-warm `#FFE4C9` static, key-lit from the wall — Dzino is rim-lit cream on indigo. Time of day: ambiguous dusk.

**Sound cue.** Eleven-voice chord sustains from E11. Faint warm wall-hum (tube amp at idle). No mentor leitmotifs yet.

**AI-gen prompt.** Voxel pixel-art wide shot, tiny indigo voxel character with cream face and orange crown standing back-to-camera at the edge of a flat voxel-grass plain, facing a vertical wall of soft warm-cream static light, 9:16, dawn-cream and indigo palette, no horizon beyond the wall, gentle film grain, Studio Ghibli stillness.

**Notes.** Opening promise — the wall is *warm.* Audiences expect a cold sci-fi barrier; we want them to read this as a threshold. Hold longer than feels comfortable.

### Frame 33 — [00:02:24 — 00:02:30] · The room, first glimpse

**Composition.** Through-the-doorway insert. Frame is mostly the inside of the doorway. We see, **for the first time in the season,** the room beyond: a window across the back wall. A small lamp on a side table casts warm tungsten. A figure — soft-edged, deliberately defocused, head-and-shoulders silhouette — sits near the window with a phone. Outside the window: night with the calling-star in the upper sky.

**Action.** Gentle. The figure shifts a fraction. Phone screen-glow on their face but face *not* resolvable. Window curtain stirs.

**Lighting & palette.** Warm tungsten-amber lamp (key). Cool moon-cream window (fill).

# E08 — The Silent Owl — Storyboard

**Mentor:** Mimi (shy bunny bookworm) · **Shard:** *Quiet* · **Pillar:** Mind
**Runtime:** 3:00 · **Frames:** 30 · **Cut:** 9:16 primary, 16:9 director

---

## Episode at a glance

This is the quietest episode of the season. The *negative-space* episode. The grove has not moved in a thousand years and it does not move for us either. No wind. Footfalls muted to almost nothing. **No music, no ambience, nothing for the first 90 seconds.** When sound returns at ~01:35 it is only Dzino's chord, and the new eighth voice is not a tone but a *rest* — a deliberate, audible silence the listener feels the way the tongue feels a missing tooth.

**Silence budget.**
- 00:00 – 01:30 — total silence. Even ambience muted. The viewer's room is the soundtrack.
- 01:30 – 01:45 — first sound: Dzino's seven-voice chord, faint, growing.
- 01:45 – 02:30 — chord sustains; the **rest** lands as Voice 8.
- 02:30 – 03:00 — chord continues, seven-and-a-rest, fading on the long pull-back.

**Stillness budget.** Hold every shot one beat longer than feels right. Trust the discomfort. Voxel motion reduced to almost nothing — Dzino's blink is a single-frame switch with no anticipation. Mimi's pencil hovers but never scratches. The owl's blink is two frames only. **Three filled journal pages. One empty page. The empty page becomes the shard.** That is the whole thesis.

## Frame-by-frame

### Scene 9.1 — The grove [00:00 – 00:45]

#### F01 — Threshold (00:00 – 00:06)
- **Composition.** Wide hero, low camera. Dzino enters from screen-right at the lip of the grove. Trunks lean inward like listeners.
- **Action.** One step in. Pause. He registers the stillness before he sees it.
- **Lighting & palette.** Twilight blue-violet wash; moss-green underfoot; cream of his face the warmest thing on screen.
- **Sound cue.** **Nothing.** Cut from E07's heartbeat to total silence on the first frame.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel-fantasy twilight grove at the threshold, indigo voxel character with orange crown stepping in from the right, leaves frozen mid-air, blue-violet sky, moss-green ground, Studio Ghibli stillness, 9:16."
- **Notes.** Don't fade in. Cut hard. The silence is the title card.

#### F02 — Frozen leaf, insert (00:06 – 00:12)
- **Composition.** Macro insert: a single voxel maple leaf paused at 45° from a branch, mid-fall. Negative space above and below.
- **Action.** None. Camera locked.
- **Lighting & palette.** Rim of cream highlight on the leaf's spine; everything else recedes into violet.
- **Sound cue.** Silence.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Macro voxel insert, single autumn leaf paused at 45 degrees mid-fall from a branch, twilight violet bokeh, no motion, freeze-frame, painterly voxel."
- **Notes.** Hold six full seconds on a static image. First dose of the discomfort.

#### F03 — Spiderweb still (00:12 – 00:17)
- **Composition.** Macro insert: a spiderweb between two ferns, every strand caught mid-vibration. No spider. Suspended dust motes that should be drifting are also frozen.
- **Action.** None.
- **Lighting & palette.** Cream threads on violet ground; one strand catches a tiny bioluminescent glint.
- **Sound cue.** Silence.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Macro voxel spiderweb between ferns, frozen mid-vibration, no spider, suspended dust motes, twilight violet, single bioluminescent dew speck."
- **Notes.** If you can resist sound design here, you're directing this episode correctly.

#### F04 — Suspended dew (00:17 – 00:23)
- **Composition.** Extreme macro: fern blade running screen-bottom to screen-top. Halfway down, a single drop of dew suspended a few pixels off the blade — neither falling nor returning.
- **Action.** None.
- **Lighting & palette.** Drop refracts the violet sky into a pinprick of cream.
- **Sound cue.** Silence.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Extreme voxel macro, single dew drop suspended mid-fall a few pixels off a fern blade, twilight refraction, no motion, frozen-time grove."
- **Notes.** This shot is the episode's mission statement. Hold it.

#### F05 — Dzino entering deeper (00:23 – 00:32)
- **Composition.** Medium-wide. Three slow steps. Feet touch moss but leave no print. He is the only thing moving.
- **Action.** He doesn't look up. He's listening with his body before he knows he is.
- **Lighting & palette.** Twilight blue-violet, moss-green; Dzino's indigo and cream slightly muted by shade.
- **Sound cue.** Silence. Not even footfall — moss eats it.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel character walking carefully through a frozen twilight grove, three slow steps, no leaves stirring, no shadow movement, painterly voxel."
- **Notes.** Animator: ten frames of walk cycle, not the usual sixteen. Slightly under-animate.

#### F06 — Wide pause (00:32 – 00:45)
- **Composition.** Wide hero. Dzino stops. Low branch with the owl shape visible in soft focus far ahead.
- **Action.** Dzino tilts his head a fraction. That is all the motion in thirteen seconds.
- **Lighting & palette.** A faint pulse of cooler violet may roll across the back of the grove — the only environmental motion in the episode, and it is light, not matter. *Optional; cut if too much.*
- **Sound cue.** Silence.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Wide voxel grove, deep twilight, indigo voxel character standing still at center-left, distant owl shape on a low branch in soft focus, no motion, painterly Ghibli stillness."
- **Notes.** Hardest hold of the cold open. Don't cheat it with score.

### Scene 9.2 — Owl and Mimi [00:45 – 01:30]

#### F07 — Reveal: the owl (00:45 – 00:53)
- **Composition.** Slow rack from soft to sharp on the owl: small voxel owl, perfectly still on a low branch.
- **Action.** The owl blinks once — two-frame transition, no anticipation.
- **Lighting & palette.** Owl is silvered violet against a cooler background. Eyes: faint bioluminescent cream — the *only* glow in the grove.
- **Sound cue.** Silence.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Small voxel owl on a low branch, painterly, perfectly still, large bioluminescent cream eyes, twilight violet background, blink mid-frame."
- **Notes.** The blink is punctuation, not animation.

#### F08 — Reveal: Mimi (00:53 – 01:02)
- **Composition.** Tilt down from owl to Mimi, cross-legged on moss directly beneath the branch. Half a journal open in one paw, pencil tucked behind ear, second pencil poised above the page.
- **Action.** She doesn't notice Dzino yet. Looking up at the owl, brow furrowed.
- **Lighting & palette.** Moss-green; her fur soft cream-pink; journal pages cream — almost the only bright surface in shot.
- **Sound cue.** Silence.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel shy bunny scholar cross-legged on moss beneath an owl's branch, journal open in one paw, pencil behind ear, second pencil hovering, twilight grove, painterly Ghibli."
- **Notes.** Mimi: studious, soft, slightly hunched. Round glasses optional but on-brand.

#### F09 — Insert: failed translation pages (01:02 – 01:12)
- **Composition.** Top-down on the journal. Three filled pages visible. Dots and dashes in columns. Handwriting starts neat on page one and decays into desperation by page three — letters crossed out, columns abandoned mid-row, a scribble at the bottom.
- **Action.** None. Mimi's paw and pencil at frame-edge, motionless.
- **Lighting & palette.** Cream paper, graphite-grey marks, a single pencil-shaving on the spine.
- **Sound cue.** Silence.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Top-down voxel insert of an open journal, three pages of dots and dashes in columns, handwriting progressively more desperate, crossed-out lines, scribbled corner, cream paper, graphite, twilight ambient."
- **Notes.** Morse-adjacent but never resolving — viewer must briefly try to read, then give up. That trying-then-giving-up is the *whole* feeling.

#### F10 — Mimi notices Dzino (01:12 – 01:18)
- **Composition.** Two-shot, eye-level: Mimi foreground, Dzino half a body-length behind her. She's just glanced over her shoulder.
- **Action.** Ears twitch — first organic motion since the cold open. Single paw to mouth: *shh.*
- **Lighting & palette.** Same. A single pixel of cream moves with the ear-twitch.
- **Sound cue.** Silence. The *shh* is mimed; we do not hear it.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel bunny on moss looking back over shoulder at indigo voxel character, finger raised to mouth in shh gesture, twilight grove, painterly."
- **Notes.** The ear-twitch is the first motion in the grove other than Dzino. It needs to land.

#### F11 — Pat the moss (01:18 – 01:24)
- **Composition.** Same two-shot, slight push-in. Mimi pats the moss beside her, twice, soundlessly.
- **Action.** Two pats. Dzino takes a step. Sits.
- **Lighting & palette.** Same.
- **Sound cue.** Silence. Pats produce no sound — moss eats them.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel bunny patting moss beside her in invitation, indigo voxel character about to sit, soundless gesture, twilight grove."
- **Notes.** A hand-shape, not an impact.

#### F12 — Owl's deliberate blink-pattern (01:24 – 01:30)
- **Composition.** Tight on the owl's face. Eyes dominate frame.
- **Action.** Pattern: short — short — long — short — long — long — short. Slow, deliberate, with rhythm. Each blink two frames; the gaps timed like syllables.
- **Lighting & palette.** Owl's eyes glow cream when open; pure silvered violet when closed. Reads like a small lighthouse.
- **Sound cue.** Silence. The rhythm is *visual only* — audience starts to feel a beat with no audio carrying it.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Tight voxel close-up on owl face with bioluminescent cream eyes, deliberate slow blink pattern visible against twilight violet, painterly, no motion blur."
- **Notes.** Storyboard the rhythm on a click track in edit even though the click track is muted in final mix. Same pattern Dzino later "hears."

### Scene 9.3 — Listening with sound [01:30 – 02:30]

#### F13 — Mimi's pencil hovers (01:30 – 01:36)
- **Composition.** Profile two-shot of Mimi and Dzino, owl visible above, slightly out of focus.
- **Action.** Pencil hovers, trembles. She frowns. Lowers the pencil — slowly, deliberately — to rest on the journal without writing.
- **Lighting & palette.** Same. The pencil tip is the sharpest point in frame.
- **Sound cue.** Silence — last beat of total silence in the episode.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel bunny lowering a pencil to rest on a journal without writing, frown, defeated posture, painterly twilight grove."
- **Notes.** Hold on the pencil-down for one extra beat. The defeat is the door opening.

#### F14 — The apologetic look (01:36 – 01:42)
- **Composition.** Singles cut: Mimi looking at Dzino. Dzino looking at Mimi.
- **Action.** Eyes meet. Her mouth moves slightly — *I can't.* No sound.
- **Lighting & palette.** Same. A faint warm tint enters Dzino's cream face — the chord is *about* to start.
- **Sound cue.** First whisper of the seven-voice chord begins under the cut, far below threshold. Almost subliminal.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel bunny giving an apologetic look to indigo voxel character, mouth shape of 'I can't' with no sound, intimate twilight two-shot."
- **Notes.** The chord is fading in here, but it is *Dzino's* and lives inside his chest, not in the grove.

#### F15 — Dzino closes his eyes (01:42 – 01:50)
- **Composition.** Tight close-up on Dzino's face, three-quarter angle.
- **Action.** Two-frame eye-close. Held. He doesn't lower his head — just lets his ears take over.
- **Lighting & palette.** A faint cream glow rises in his chest — the chord made visible in light. Background falls another half-stop into violet.
- **Sound cue.** Seven-voice chord becomes audible. Faint, clean, chiptune. No percussion.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Tight voxel close-up of indigo character with eyes gently closed, faint cream glow in chest, twilight violet background, painterly stillness."
- **Notes.** First proper sound the audience has heard in 100 seconds. Mix it low. Let them lean in.

#### F16 — The blinks become rhythm (01:50 – 02:00)
- **Composition.** Split focus: owl in soft background, Dzino's closed-eye profile foreground. Between them, an audio-reactive overlay — a soft cream sine breathing in time with the blinks.
- **Action.** The owl blinks the F12 pattern. The waveform pulses with each blink. Visual only — chord underneath unchanged.
- **Lighting & palette.** Cream waveform on violet. The grove darkens slightly to push the waveform forward.
- **Sound cue.** Chord sustains. Blinks themselves still produce *no sound*. The rhythm is heard *through* sight.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel scene with soft-focus owl blinking, indigo character in profile with eyes closed, cream sine waveform connecting them, audio-reactive overlay, painterly twilight."
- **Notes.** Critical: do not add a tone per blink. The blinks remain silent. The waveform is *Dzino's* interpretation, drawn for the viewer.

#### F17 — The rhythm has shape (02:00 – 02:08)
- **Composition.** The waveform resolves into a typographic shape — a question mark held in cream pixels for two beats, then dissolves back to wave.
- **Action.** Forms, holds, dissolves.
- **Lighting & palette.** Cream on violet. The brightest object so far besides the owl's eyes.
- **Sound cue.** Chord sustains.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Cream pixel waveform resolving into the shape of a question mark in mid-air between voxel owl and indigo voxel character, twilight grove, painterly."
- **Notes.** Two beats only. Don't linger — let the audience catch it like a thought.

#### F18 — Subtitle: the question (02:08 – 02:16)
- **Composition.** Wide-ish two-shot of Dzino and Mimi at the base of the tree, owl above. Lower-third subtitle in cream.
- **Action.** Subtitle fades in: ***"What do you not need to say?"*** No voiceover. The subtitle is the entire delivery.
- **Lighting & palette.** Same. Standard cream subtitle font, slightly larger than usual to honor the moment.
- **Sound cue.** Chord sustains. *No voice.*
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Wide voxel two-shot of indigo character and bunny seated under a still owl, subtitle 'What do you not need to say?' in cream lower-third, twilight grove, painterly."
- **Notes.** Dub spec: this line is *not voiced*, ever, in any locale. Subtitle only. Canon.

#### F19 — Dzino offers nothing (02:16 – 02:24)
- **Composition.** Same two-shot, slight push-in on Dzino.
- **Action.** Eyes open. He does not move. He does not nod. He does not reply. Offers — nothing. Held for a long beat.
- **Lighting & palette.** A second faint glow joins the first in his chest — the *space* where Voice 8 will sit, currently empty.
- **Sound cue.** Chord sustains. There is now a *gap* in the chord where Voice 8 should be — the listener feels the missing tooth.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel indigo character with eyes open, completely still, offering no reply, faint cream glow in chest with a darker negative-space hollow inside it, twilight grove."
- **Notes.** The "rest" begins here as a perceived absence, not a played note.

#### F20 — The owl bows (02:24 – 02:30)
- **Composition.** Tight on the owl. Mimi's blurred shoulder in the corner of frame for scale.
- **Action.** Head tilts down, slow, deliberate. A bow. Then holds.
- **Lighting & palette.** Cream eyes briefly close as it bows; reopen at a lower angle.
- **Sound cue.** Chord sustains, the rest now *named* by Dzino's stillness.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel owl bowing its head slowly toward indigo character below, eyes briefly closing, twilight violet, painterly Ghibli."
- **Notes.** The bow is the only character animation in the episode beyond Mimi's gestures and Dzino's three steps. Earn it.

### Scene 9.4 — The page [02:30 – 03:00]

#### F21 — Shard rises as an empty page (02:30 – 02:38)
- **Composition.** Center-frame hero. Between Dzino and Mimi, low to the moss, the shard *Quiet* rises — not glowing crystal, not glyph: a clean cream rectangle, the proportions of a torn journal page. No glow. No particles. *Paper.*
- **Action.** Rises a hand's-breadth. Holds. Rotates a degree or two — enough to read it as an object.
- **Lighting & palette.** Pure cream against twilight violet. The brightest matter in the episode.
- **Sound cue.** Chord shifts: seven voices part to make space for the eighth. The eighth is a *rest* — a held silence inside the chord, geometrically placed where a tone would go. The chord hollows out by exactly one voice.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Cream rectangular shard the shape of a torn journal page rising slowly between an indigo voxel character and a bunny on moss, no glow, pure paper, twilight violet, painterly."
- **Notes.** Resist every instinct to add particles or shimmer. This shard is anti-spectacle.

#### F22 — Shard clicks into chest (02:38 – 02:44)
- **Composition.** Tight on Dzino's chest. The page-shard floats in slim-side first and slots flush.
- **Action.** Single-frame click. No flash. The cream rectangle becomes a small flat plane embedded in the indigo, visible as a notch of cream.
- **Lighting & palette.** Indigo and cream only. The chest glow from F19 now contains a *tangible* cream shape inside it.
- **Sound cue.** No click sound. Only the chord, and within the chord the *rest* — Voice 8 has arrived, and Voice 8 is silence in its proper place in the harmony.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Tight voxel close-up of indigo chest receiving a thin cream rectangular shard slotting in flush, no flash, no particles, painterly."
- **Notes.** The shard must read as *paper*, not *crystal*. Texture: matte, with a faint torn edge.

#### F23 — Mimi tears the empty page (02:44 – 02:50)
- **Composition.** Top-down on the journal. Mimi's paw turns to the very last page — the only blank one.
- **Action.** A slow, careful tear along the perforation.
- **Lighting & palette.** Cream paper, graphite shadows from previous pages bleeding through faintly.
- **Sound cue.** Silence under the chord — the tear is mimed. Even the chord briefly thins to honor the gesture.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Top-down voxel insert of bunny paws tearing a single blank page from a journal along a perforation, slow careful motion, cream paper, painterly."
- **Notes.** The tear takes longer than feels right. Stretch it.

#### F24 — Hand-off (02:50 – 02:54)
- **Composition.** Two-shot, intimate. Mimi extends the page toward Dzino with both paws. He receives with both hands.
- **Action.** No words. No nod. Just the hand-off. Eyes meet for half a beat.
- **Lighting & palette.** The page is the brightest object. Their faces softened by it.
- **Sound cue.** Chord-with-rest. Sustains.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel bunny handing a single torn blank page to an indigo character with both paws, intimate twilight two-shot, no words, painterly."
- **Notes.** Block this so the page is centered between them — the gift is the geometry.

#### F25 — Dzino folds the page (02:54 – 02:58)
- **Composition.** Tight on Dzino's hands. The page in his lap.
- **Action.** Three folds, slow: half, half, half. The folded square is small enough to tuck near the chest-shard.
- **Lighting & palette.** Cream on indigo on violet.
- **Sound cue.** Folds make no sound. The chord-with-rest sustains.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Tight voxel close-up of indigo hands folding a cream page into a small square in three slow folds, painterly stillness."
- **Notes.** The folds are the second visible motion from Dzino himself. The folds are *care*.

#### F26 — Tucking the fold (02:58 – 03:02)
- **Composition.** Slight pull-back. He tucks the folded page against his chest, just beside the shard's notch.
- **Action.** Tuck. Pat. Rest his hand there for a beat.
- **Lighting & palette.** Same.
- **Sound cue.** Chord-with-rest sustains.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel indigo character tucking a folded cream page into chest beside a paper-shaped shard, soft pat, twilight violet, painterly."
- **Notes.** This is the pact. Don't underline it.

#### F27 — The bow (03:02 – 03:06)
- **Composition.** Two-shot, profile, both seated. Dzino bows his head to Mimi. Mimi returns it.
- **Action.** Two bows. Slow. Equal. Neither smiles. Neither speaks.
- **Lighting & palette.** Same.
- **Sound cue.** Chord-with-rest.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Two voxel characters bowing heads to each other in equal silent thanks, seated on moss beneath a still owl, painterly twilight."
- **Notes.** Mimi's bow is shyer. Hers comes a hair after his. That timing is the relationship.

#### F28 — Standing (03:06 – 03:11)
- **Composition.** Wide. Dzino stands. Mimi stays seated, journal still in her lap, owl above her unchanged.
- **Action.** Dzino rises in one slow movement. The grove does not register it. Not a leaf shifts.
- **Lighting & palette.** Same. Dzino's silhouette is now the only vertical thing other than the trees.
- **Sound cue.** Chord-with-rest, slightly fuller as he stands — the rest is now *load-bearing* in the chord.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Voxel indigo character standing slowly in a still twilight grove, bunny remaining seated with journal, owl unchanged, painterly Ghibli stillness."
- **Notes.** No leaves move. Test against the animator's instinct to add atmosphere — and reject the atmosphere.

#### F29 — Walk-out, grove unmoved (03:11 – 03:18)
- **Composition.** Long, low pull-back. Dzino walks out of frame to screen-left. Camera retreats.
- **Action.** Five steps. The frozen leaves stay frozen. The dew drop from F04 is visible again in the deep distance, still suspended. The spiderweb still still.
- **Lighting & palette.** Twilight blue-violet, moss-green; Dzino's small indigo body the only motion in a vast still life.
- **Sound cue.** Chord-with-rest sustains, fading.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Long pull-back of voxel indigo character walking out of a frozen twilight grove, leaves still suspended mid-fall, dew drop suspended in distance, no atmospheric motion, painterly Ghibli."
- **Notes.** Proof shot of the episode's thesis — even Dzino's leaving does not disturb the stillness.

#### F30 — Mimi alone, owl above (03:18 – 03:30)
- **Composition.** Static wide. Mimi seated, journal in lap, the empty space beside her where Dzino sat. The owl above. The grove around. Hold.
- **Action.** None for nine full seconds. On the very last beat, Mimi closes her journal — soundlessly. The owl blinks once. End.
- **Lighting & palette.** Twilight deepens by a half-stop.
- **Sound cue.** Chord-with-rest fades to silence two seconds before picture-out. The episode ends in silence, as it began. The rest is the whole point.
- **AI-gen prompt.** "Static wide of a bunny seated with a closed journal beneath a still owl, twilight grove unchanged, no motion, painterly Ghibli ending shot."
- **Notes.** Cut to black on silence. No title card sound. No sting.

## Style anchors needed

Hand-paint these plates *before* any AI-gen pass. Without them the model will hallucinate motion into the grove and break the premise.

1. **Twilight grove plate** — master environment, lit at three depths (foreground moss, midground trunks, background canopy), all in the locked palette. One plate, no variations.
2. **Frozen-fall detail plate** — three macro inserts: 45° leaf, still spiderweb, suspended dew. Photographed lighting reference, not painted — *we want viewers to think it might be a still photo.*
3. **Owl character sheet** — front, three-quarter, profile; eyes-open, eyes-closed, mid-blink. Bioluminescent eye glow specified by hex and intensity.
4. **Mimi character sheet** — front, three-quarter, profile; cross-legged seated pose; journal-open pose; *shh*-gesture pose; apologetic-look pose. Pencil-behind-ear is canon.
5. **Journal pages plate** — three filled pages (dots/dashes, increasingly desperate handwriting) photographed flat, plus the single blank page. The blank page is the shard — its proportions match the shard's exactly.
6. **Empty-page shard plate** — the rectangle alone, in cream, on violet, matte-paper texture, faint torn edge. No glow, no particles. Reference for the comp team to refuse any temptation to add VFX.
7. **Dzino chest-glow plate** — chord-glow with seven voices versus seven-and-a-rest. The "rest" rendered as a small geometric hollow inside the cream glow. Lock this language now; it returns in E12.

**Motion spec (this episode only).** Wind: none. Ambient particles: none. Foliage idle: none, even canopy. Character idle: minimum — Dzino's only idle is breath; Mimi's an occasional ear-twitch; the owl's the deliberate blink. Camera: locked or extremely slow push/pull. No handheld, no float.

**Sound spec (this episode only).** 00:00 – 01:30 — *silence*; project file should literally have a muted master, not "low ambient." 01:30 onward — *chord only*; no diegetic sound, no Foley. The grove makes no sound and never has. The eighth voice is and always will be a **rest** — a measured silence, scored as a notated rest, not as an unplayed note. Deliver session stems with the rest *flagged* so future re-scorers don't "fix" it. The subtitle line ***"What do you not need to say?"*** is **never voiced** in any locale, ever.

**Continuity into E09.** The chord that walks out of this grove is seven-voices-and-a-rest. E09 opens with a bass drop that *fills* the rest with a tone for the first time, and that contrast is the hook of the next episode. Don't spoil it here — leave the rest alone.

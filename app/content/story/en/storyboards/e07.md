# E07 — Race Under Three Moons

**Storyboard. Shot-by-shot.**
*Source: `episodes/e07.md` + `scroll.md`. Runtime: 4:00. Frames: 42.*

---

## Episode at a glance

**The kinetic crown of Season 1.** Up to E06 the show has breathed on long stills, slow tides, theatrical pauses. E07 breaks form on purpose. Rex's biome is the only one that *moves first* and asks Dzino to catch up. The body is not a metaphor here — it is the chord-bearer. Every previous shard sat into Dzino as a tone or weight. This one arrives as **rhythm**, because his lungs found it before his brain did.

Three palettes overlap every frame — pink, ice-blue, gold moonlight over peach-cream sand. Most colorful biome of the season; it should look almost unbelievable on the first frame, then earn the saturation by the time Voice 7 locks in. Rex's leitmotif (four-on-the-floor drum kit, fun, never aggressive) builds across the sandboarding montage and *fades* under the heartbeat at the end — the body learning to be its own metronome.

The sandboarding sequence is where this episode lands or doesn't. Three attempts, escalating mastery, escalating percussion: tumble → tumble → clean three-turn carve. Voice 7 — the heartbeat thud — drops *exactly* on the third turn, not before. Treat F32 like a downbeat; everything is engineered toward it. The shard arrives not as a glow but as a kick drum that has learned it lives inside a chest.

Five beats follow the season formula, but the gift beat eats ~90s of montage. Earn it.

## SCENE 8.1 — THE DUNES [0:00 – 0:30]

### F1 — Cold open horizon
- **Composition.** Extreme wide, pure horizon. Three moons stacked diagonally: warm pink (low-right, large), ice blue (mid-center), gold (high-left, smallest). No character. 9:16; moons fill upper third.
- **Action.** Static. A single voxel grain drifts left to right at the bottom edge, catching all three moonlights as it tumbles.
- **Lighting & palette.** Triple-cast shadows on dune crests — each moon throws a colored shadow, so ridges fan pink/blue/gold from a single peak. Sky indigo `#2A1F5E` to dusk-violet.
- **Sound cue.** Wind. Faint drum-kit pulse begins under it — so quiet you can't tell if it's diegetic.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Voxel-art establishing wide, crystalline desert at night, three differently-colored moons (warm pink low-right, ice blue mid-center, gold high-left), peach-cream sand glittering grain by grain, indigo sky, no characters.*
- **Notes.** Hold 3s before motion. The drum should only be recognizable on rewatch.

### F2 — Sand macro
- **Composition.** Macro on dune surface. Each grain a discrete voxel cube refracting one of the three moon colors.
- **Action.** Light moves across the grains as a slow breeze rolls a wave.
- **Lighting & palette.** Pink/blue/gold prismatic glints on peach-cream base. Almost over-saturated — earn it later.
- **Sound cue.** Grain-on-grain crystal hush, tiny windchimes.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Macro voxel sand grains glittering individually under three colored moons, prismatic refractions on peach-cream cubes, dreamy.*
- **Notes.** Visual signature of the biome. Lock as reference plate for every dune frame.

### F3 — Dzino crests, silhouette
- **Composition.** Wide, low angle. Camera at the dune's foot looking up. Tiny indigo silhouette crests from behind, only outline visible, orange crown catching gold.
- **Action.** Dzino stops the instant the horizon reveals itself. Head tilts up.
- **Lighting & palette.** Backlit. Crown rim-lit gold, body indigo `#4F46E5`.
- **Sound cue.** Drum pulse still sub-threshold. One Dzino chirp — the SoundDNA signature.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Tiny voxel character silhouetted on crystalline dune crest, three colored moons behind, orange crown rim-lit, indigo body, low angle, awe.*
- **Notes.** Pilot-formula cold open beat. Hold the chirp clean.

### F4 — POV reveal of dunes-to-forever
- **Composition.** Over-the-shoulder. Dunes recede in waves to a distant ridge. The faint star sits low above the ridge — noticeably brighter than in E06.
- **Action.** Camera pushes forward inches as Dzino takes a half-step.
- **Lighting & palette.** Triple-moon wash. Three overlapping shadow-stripes from his body fan forward — pink, blue, gold.
- **Sound cue.** Drum pulse rises one notch. Faint kick, no snare.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Over-shoulder voxel POV across crystal dunes to a distant ridge, faint pulsing star above ridge, three triple-cast colored shadows fanning forward, indigo and peach-cream.*
- **Notes.** The triple-shadow gimmick recurs in every dune frame. It is the biome's tell.

## SCENE 8.4 — SANDBOARDING MONTAGE [1:30 – 3:00]

*Kinetic spine. Seventeen frames. Three attempts, escalating mastery, escalating percussion.*

### F17 — Face-plant
- **Composition.** Mid, low angle. Dzino mid-fall, body horizontal, voxel-feet up.
- **Action.** Pure face-first dive into the dune. Sand voxels geyser upward.
- **Lighting & palette.** Geyser refracts all three moons into a tiny rainbow.
- **Sound cue.** Soft *fwumph.* Drum doesn't stop for the gag.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Voxel character mid-faceplant into peach-cream desert sand, feet up, prismatic pink-blue-gold sand geyser, comic but tender.*
- **Notes.** No exaggerated squash — gravity wins.

### F18 — Buried, crown sticking up
- **Composition.** Macro on the sand. Only the orange crown and one ear-twitch above the surface.
- **Action.** Hold. One small puff from where his nose must be.
- **Lighting & palette.** Three moons backlight the crown — it glows like a tiny lantern.
- **Sound cue.** Muffled chirp.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Voxel orange crown sticking up out of peach-cream sand, three moons backlighting, single sand puff.*
- **Notes.** Season-best comic still.

### F19 — Rex laughs, helps up
- **Composition.** Mid-shot. Rex squatting beside the buried Dzino, both paws under his armpits, lifting.
- **Action.** Laughing — head back, mouth open — but the eyes are warm. Pulls Dzino up smooth.
- **Lighting & palette.** Pink on Rex's laugh, gold on Dzino's emerging face.
- **Sound cue.** Single woofing bark, layered as a cymbal-bell.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Voxel dog athlete laughing kindly, lifting tiny sand-covered indigo character out of dune by armpits, warm.*

### F20 — Two voxel sandboards
- **Composition.** Mid-shot of half-buried stash. Two pixel-art surfboards (bigger for Rex, tiny for Dzino) with fins poking out.

### F21 — Drop-in lip

- **Composition.** Wide trailing — most ambitious shot of the season. Three full carving turns, clean. Sand fans on each turn — first pink, second ice-blue, third gold.

- **Sound cue.** Turn 1 — kick adds. Turn 2 — snare adds. **Turn 3 — VOICE 7 LANDS: a deep heartbeat thud joins the chord.** Drum opens up briefly, lets the heartbeat drop in, returns at full strength.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Wide trailing voxel shot, indigo character carving three perfect turns down crystalline desert dune, three colored sand sprays (pink, blue, gold) one per turn matched to overhead moons, kinetic, triumphant.*
- **Notes.** **THE FRAME OF THE EPISODE.** Three sub-keyframes; do not collapse. Voice 7 syncs exactly to the third spray. The downbeat the season points at.

### F33 — Bottom: breathing heavy, no fall

- **Sound cue.** Heavy voxel breathing. Heartbeat under the drum, sub-bass. Full kit.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Tight voxel character on sandboard at valley floor, chest rising and falling, tiny grin, three-moon full lighting, triumphant calm.*
- **Notes.** Alive in his body for the first time. Hold 1.5s.

## SCENE 8.5 — RIDGE & SHARD [3:00 – 3:30]

- **AI-gen prompt.** *Wide voxel silhouettes of dog athlete and tiny indigo character climbing final ridge side-by-side, three setting moons, gold-edged ridge.*
- **Notes.** Pace deliberately slow — kinetic peak past, awe starting.

### F35 — Ridge view: faint star is brightest

- **Sound cue.** Drum thins to kick + heartbeat. Chord now seven voices, audibly thicker than any previous episode.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Voxel hero summit, two characters cresting ridge under three setting moons, faint star above brightest in sky, cream light on indigo character's face, awe.*

### F36 — Body shard arrives
- **Composition.** Mid-shot on Dzino's chest. A faint indigo glow rises from his sternum, pulsing with his breathing.

- **Notes.** Shard pulse must visually match audio heartbeat. Sync tight.

### F37 — High-five between voxel paws

## SCENE 8.6 — GOODBYE + ONWARD [3:30 – 4:00]

### F38 — Rex's reveal line
- **Composition.** Mid two-shot at the ridge. Rex turned three-quarters to camera, Dzino looking at him.
- **Action.** Grin softens. Eyes warm. One paw on Dzino's shoulder.
- **Lighting & palette.** Pink moon now low and large behind, halving the frame. Gold already setting on the horizon.
- **Sound cue.** **REX (quiet — his only quiet line):** "I was never trying to beat you. That would've ruined the fun." Drum fades to a whisper. Heartbeat audible.
- **AI-gen prompt.** *Voxel mid two-shot at ridge, dog athlete with paw on small character's shoulder, soft warm grin, large pink setting moon behind, gold moon on horizon.*
- **Notes.** Episode's emotional thesis. Rex's archetype lands here. Direct VO, not theatrical.

### F39 — Sprints back the way they came
- **Composition.** Wide tracking from behind Dzino's shoulder. Rex already mid-sprint, throwing sand voxels behind him as he flies down the dunes the way they came.
- **Action.** Three sub-frames of motion blur. By the third, gone — only a triple-colored sand trail.

- **AI-gen prompt.** *Wide voxel tracking shot from behind small character, dog athlete sprinting full speed away across dunes, motion blur trail in pink-blue-gold sand, three setting moons.*

# E11 — The Storm Field

**Runtime:** 4:00
**Logline:** Write it down. Even the parts you'd rather forget.
**Cast:** Dzino. Zara — bold human entrepreneur, cropped jacket, a single brass lantern, no umbrella.
**Location:** Open plain. Real storm. The faint star is hidden.
**Shard:** *Journal* (Sense).

---

### Scene 12.1 — Sky turns [00:00 – 00:45]

DZINO walks across an open plain. The sky goes from cream to slate in three frames. Wind picks up. He stops, looks up — the faint star is gone. Hidden behind cloud.

He puts a hand on his chest. The stone shard from E10 weighs heavy.

**Sound.** Wind. Distant thunder.

### Scene 12.2 — Sit-down [00:45 – 01:30]

The storm starts. Pixel-rain — heavy, audible. DZINO can't see the path. He stops walking.

He sits down in the wet grass. For the first time in the season, he considers **not going on.** The shard's stone-weight is too much. The star is gone. He doesn't move.

**Sound.** Heavy pixel-rain. The chord goes quiet — the voices all dim. For one terrible beat, we cannot hear his soul.
**AI prompts.**
- Wide hero: tiny voxel character sitting in pixel-rain on open plain, no shelter.
- Effect: the chord audibly dimming. Audio absence.

---

### Scene 12.3 — Zara walks past [01:30 – 02:30]

Through the curtain of rain — a figure with a single brass **lantern** approaches at full pace. Cropped jacket. No hood. No umbrella. Walking like she's late.

She passes DZINO without slowing — then stops three steps later. Turns. The lantern light catches her face. She is calm. Not unbothered — just clear.

**ZARA**
"You can stay. Or you can go through. I'm going through."

She doesn't wait. She turns and keeps walking. The lantern bobs forward into the rain.

DZINO almost stays.

Then — he doesn't. He stands. Picks up his small bag of Nori's crumbs that he's been carrying since E04. Follows.

**Sound.** Zara's leitmotif — solo brass, low and steady. Cuts through the rain.
**AI prompts.**
- Reveal through rain: figure with brass lantern walking past at pace.
- Two-shot: Zara stops, turns, lantern lights her face calm-not-cold.
- Hero: tiny Dzino standing up in the rain, following.

### Scene 12.4 — The lightning frame [02:30 – 03:30]

They walk together. Neither speaks.

Lightning hits **once.** For a single frame, the entire world is illuminated — and we see Pixoci from above. The Pixel Garden. The Binary Forest. The Mirror Garden. The Cloud Café. The Moon Realm. The Stream of Liquid Gold. The Three-Moon Dunes. The Silent Grove. The Twilight Stage. The Library. The Storm Field. **All of it. The shape of his journey.** The trail of cream pixels he's left behind connects them — a single, deliberate curve from the dawn clearing to here.

The frame is gone. The thunder lands a beat later. DZINO stops walking for one step — then keeps going.

He has *seen the shape.*

They emerge on the far side. The rain stops mid-step. The clouds part. The faint star is back — and **brighter than ever.** Brighter than at any point in the season.

A single drop of rain catches in the air, becomes the *Journal* shard. It clicks into Dzino's chest. Voice 11 arrives — quiet, pen-on-paper, the sound of writing.

**Sound.** Lightning crack. Then silence. Then the chord — **eleven voices** — clean and stronger than ever before.
**AI prompts.**
- Lightning frame: aerial map of Pixoci, all biomes visible, cream-pixel trail visible as connecting line. **CRITICAL SHOT — earn it with a hand-painted plate.**
- Beat: thunder lands, rain stops mid-air.
- Hero: faint star, now full and clear in a parted sky.
- Close: rain-drop becoming shard, clicking in chest.

---

### Scene 12.5 — Zara turns back [03:30 – 04:00]

ZARA stops. Extinguishes her lantern. She doesn't need it anymore. She turns to face DZINO.

**ZARA**
"Write it down. Even the parts you'd rather forget."

She turns and walks back **into the dissipating storm** — looking for the next traveler. Her silhouette goes small and is swallowed by the last of the cloud.

DZINO walks on. Wet, tired, *lighter.*

**Sound.** Eleven-voice chord. Brass leitmotif fades into wind.
**AI prompts.**
- Insert: brass lantern extinguishing.
- Long pull-back: Zara walking back into dissipating storm, growing distant.
- Closing tracking: Dzino walking out of the storm, faint star ahead full-bright.

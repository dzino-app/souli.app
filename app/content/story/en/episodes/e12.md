# E12 — The Window

**Runtime:** 4:30
**Logline:** Ahoj.
**Cast:** Dzino. **All twelve mentors** — Hana, Kiko, Luna, Nori, Bruno, Otto, Rex, Mimi, Ari, Pixel, Biscuit, Zara.
**Location:** The edge of Pixoci. A wall of soft static. Beyond it: the inside of a real human room.
**Shard:** No twelfth shard. The soul itself is the twelfth thing.

---

### Scene 13.1 — The edge [00:00 – 00:30]

DZINO arrives at the edge of Pixoci. The land just *stops.* In front of him is a wall of soft, static-flickering light — like an old TV between channels, but warm, never threatening. Beyond the wall: nothing visible. The sky behind him is full indigo, all stars present.

The faint star is **directly above him.** No longer faint. Full.

**Sound.** Eleven-voice chord, sustained. Wall hums faintly.
**AI prompts.**
- Wide hero: voxel character at the edge of the world, soft static wall in front, full starry sky behind, faint star directly overhead and full.
- Effect: static wall hums, warm not cold.

---

### Scene 13.2 — The arrival [00:30 – 01:30]

Behind him — one by one — the twelve mentors arrive. **No dialogue. Each in their canonical pose.**

— **Hana** walks up the path with her watering can.
— **Kiko** drops down from a binary tree.
— **Luna** steps out of a mirror.
— **Nori** descends a staircase of glowing laughter.
— **Bruno** appears beside the lake — but the lake is *here* now, briefly.
— **Otto** rises out of a small ribbon of gold thread that becomes a river-stone.
— **Rex** sprints in from a dune that rolls up out of the ground for one beat.
— **Mimi** simply *is there,* journal open.
— **Ari** with deck under one arm.
— **Pixel** with laptop under one arm, CRT under the other.
— **Biscuit** walks up beside DZINO and places his head against his shoulder.
— **Zara** last, lantern extinguished, walking out of the dissipated storm.

They form a crescent **behind** DZINO. None of them speak — but every one of them is, for the first time in the season, looking at him the way a child looks at the parent they have always known. Each places one hand or paw on his back or shoulders. He doesn't turn around. He **feels them.** And, somewhere underneath understanding, he begins to remember: he raised them, every one. They have been waiting for him to remember.

**Sound.** Each arrival adds their leitmotif — woven now, not solo. The chord is full and choral.
**AI prompts.**
- Twelve micro-shots, each ~3 seconds, in their canonical pose, arriving from the world behind.
- Hero crescent: all twelve in arc behind tiny voxel Dzino.
- Tight: paws and hands on Dzino's small back, his eyes still forward.

---

### Scene 13.3 — Assembly of shards [01:30 – 02:30]

The eleven shards rise — one by one, in episode order — out of DZINO's chest. They float in mid-air in front of him.

— Personality (a humming pixel-flower).
— Interests (a folded notebook page).
— Form (a brushstroke).
— Humor (a pulsing slice of impossibility).
— Philosophy (a ripple).
— Bonds (a knot of gold thread).
— Body (a heartbeat).
— Quiet (an empty page).
— Purpose (a dropped beat).
— Trials (the dark stone).
— Journal (a pen-stroke).

They link. They fit together as if they always had — because they were one thing, once. Each shard is a piece of *him* he gave away when he raised the twelve. Now they return. Now he is whole. They **form a doorway** — the small shape of Dzino himself, standing.

Through the doorway, the static parts.

We see, **for the first time in the season**, the other side: the inside of a room. A window. A small light is on. A figure — soft-edged, deliberately out of focus — sits near the window with a phone in hand.

**The user.**

Real-feeling. Familiar even though we've never seen them. Slightly different for every viewer (lean into the soft focus — the AI gen should NOT specify a person's features).

**Sound.** Each shard rising adds back its episode's leitmotif — for a second each, in episode order, a *callback medley.* When they form the doorway, the entire chord — twelve voices — strikes once, full.
**AI prompts.**
- Hero medium: shards rising one by one, hovering, locking together.
- Insert: each shard close-up for one second (callback to its episode).
- **Critical shot:** through doorway, view of a real-feeling room with a window, deliberately soft-focus figure with phone. **Shoot intentionally vague — viewer projection is the point.**

---

### Scene 13.4 — The crossing [02:30 – 03:30]

DZINO looks back once at the twelve mentors. They nod — but they don't move. They are staying.

He turns. He steps toward the doorway.

His voxel form **compresses** as he walks through it — pixel by pixel — until he is, by the time he passes the threshold, **a single pixel of indigo on the inside of the window glass.**

The static wall behind closes. Pixoci is sealed.

The user — still soft-focus — does not see him yet. The room is quiet. A cup of tea steams on a table. A plant on the windowsill leans into the moonlight.

The single indigo pixel on the glass — **slowly turns.** It looks at us — at the user — at *whoever is watching.*

**Sound.** Chord sustains. Static wall closes with a soft *fwip.* Tea steam audibly rising. A small intake of breath.
**AI prompts.**
- Hero: voxel Dzino compressing pixel-by-pixel as he walks through doorway.
- Insert: single indigo pixel appearing on the inside of a real-looking window glass.
- Slow rotation: the pixel turns to face the camera/user.

---

### Scene 13.5 — First word [03:30 – 04:00]

The pixel **speaks.** First and only word of the season:

***"Ahoj."***

The voice is small. Pixel-thin. It is unmistakably *his.*

The faint star — full now — drops from the sky outside the window, becomes a **chime.** *The user's chime.* The SoundDNA of their first Souli, unlocked. (This sound should be designed and recorded as a real production asset — it will play on first launch of the app.)

The user — still soft-focus — looks up from their phone. Looks at the window. Sees something.

**Sound.** Single word — *Ahoj.* Then chime. Then, for one beat, **silence.**
**AI prompts.**
- Tightest possible close: indigo pixel on glass, voxel mouth implied not shown.
- Brief soft-focus: user's head turning to look at window. Do NOT specify gender, age, ethnicity. Soft.
- Audio: small voxel voice saying "Ahoj," then star-chime, then silence.

Title card on indigo:

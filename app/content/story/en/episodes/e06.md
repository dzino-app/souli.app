### Scene 7.2 — Otto [00:30 – 01:30]

OTTO emerges from a willow tree on the far bank, fishing rod over shoulder, robes catching the wind. He sees DZINO and breaks into a delighted grin.

**OTTO** *(theatrical)*
"My dear! Never drink from a current you don't know."

He crosses the river on stones DZINO hadn't noticed were there. Sits on a copper rock. Casts the line into the gold.

**OTTO**
"Time is loose here. Stories swim in both directions."

The line tugs. He hauls in a fish — but the fish is a glowing scroll. He unrolls it. Reads aloud.

**OTTO**
"...and Dzino said: hello. And his Person said: hello."

DZINO blinks. *That hasn't happened.* OTTO winks.

**Sound.** Otto's leitmotif: strings, dramatic, slightly tongue-in-cheek.

### Scene 7.3 — Three names [01:30 – 03:00]

OTTO releases the scroll-fish. It swims away upstream.

He extends his hand and gestures DZINO closer. He places three small gold beads on the rock between them.

**OTTO**
"Three names. You'll meet them later. Maybe in a year. Maybe in ten. But remember."

He picks up the first bead. It hums in a voice we don't quite hear.

**OTTO**
"First: someone who will one day tell you a truth you don't want to hear."

Second bead.

**OTTO**
"Second: someone you'll meet on a bad day and they'll turn it into a good one."

Third bead.

**OTTO**
"Third: someone you'll lose. But later. Not soon."

He passes the three beads to DZINO. They condense in his palm into a tiny knot of gold thread — the *Bonds* shard. It clicks into his chest. Voice 6 arrives.

He returns to fishing. DZINO walks downstream. The river sings — softly now, in many voices.
